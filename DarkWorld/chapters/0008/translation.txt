"Excuse me, is this a test?" Wei Li asked tentatively, looking at Kui Xin.

"You can think of it that way," Kui Xin replied.

The meeting room went dead quiet.

Golden Ring ruled himself out first. Come on, he had nothing to hide. He was a player, and back in the First World he'd reported his identity and joined Black Snake's personal forum long ago. If anyone had a clean background, it was him.

There were seven people around the conference table. Four were players, and the other three were obviously full members of the Sea Without Light.

Fer-de-lance was clearly the one calling the shots, a cut above everyone else. The two seats beside her were taken. One held a pitch-black blob of shadow with so little presence you could barely tell it was there. From his outline, the person in the other seat was a man, and he hadn't said a single word.

Golden Ring turned his attention to the other candidates.

There was a woman dressed in streetwear and a man with a foreign face. The last one was East Asian, like him.

"Let me introduce myself. My codename is Golden Ring," he said, speaking up first. "Since there's a good chance we'll be working together, we should get to know each other."

In a game of "find the spy," everyone has to get to know each other first. If nobody says a word, there's no way to work out who the spy is.

"Wei Li." Wei Li gave her real name without a second thought. "Unemployed drifter. Nothing much worth mentioning."

"Samanto. Bounty hunter, wanted by the Federation." Samanto glanced at the others around the table.

Lancet spoke up last. "Lancet. Also a bounty hunter."

Once everyone had given their names, Golden Ring smiled. "The first test the interviewer has set us is to guess which of us four is a traitor-in-waiting. Sitting here in a standoff won't get us anywhere, so how about each of us says one thing to clear our name? Saves us all from suspecting each other."

Wei Li stared at him. "You first."

"Fine," said Golden Ring. "I've got a grudge against the Federation."

Wei Li frowned. "That's it?"

"That's it." Golden Ring spread his hands.

"Then I'll go," said Wei Li. "I've got a grudge against the plutocrat families."

With the two of them done, everyone naturally turned to Samanto.

"I'm a wanted man. I've spent years on the run, hiding wherever I could, and I've made plenty of enemies. Whoever I end up working for, it won't be the Federation or the plutocrats." Samanto spread his hands.

The three who had answered looked at Lancet, the last one left.

Lancet thought for a moment. "I have reasons why I have to join the Sea Without Light. I'd rather not share them with you. All I can say is that I won't be a traitor."

"Everyone sitting here has reasons why they have to join the Sea Without Light," said Wei Li. "People join an organization like this for one of two reasons. Either they have huge ambitions, or they've run out of options."

Samanto sounded amused. "Ms. Wei Li, your view of the Sea Without Light seems a little negative."

"You misunderstand. What I mean is that the Sea Without Light is the only choice any of us here had." Wei Li's expression didn't change. "If we'd had any other option, none of us would have ended up here."

Golden Ring knew exactly what she meant. Lancet, across the table, had more or less hinted at the same thing just now.

If life was going well, who would choose to live on a knife's edge?

But Samanto… he didn't seem to have been cornered at all. He seemed to have applied simply because the idea appealed to him.

"Hey, Samanto," Golden Ring said thoughtfully. "Mind telling me why you chose the Sea Without Light?"

"That question takes me right back to the interview," Samanto said, with a mild jab. "Golden Ring, you don't seem to be a full member of the Sea Without Light. You haven't even joined yet, and you're already playing interviewer?"

"I'm asking because I have doubts about you," Golden Ring said bluntly. "You don't have to answer. I'll just bump you up my list of suspects."

"Sounds to me like you've got something to hide. Only a traitor with a guilty conscience would be in such a hurry to pin it on someone else." Samanto looked him straight in the eye. "I already told the interviewer why I came to the Sea Without Light. I don't owe you an answer. Think whatever you like."

Golden Ring slowly raised his head and looked at Fer-de-lance in the middle of the table.

Fer-de-lance made no move to stop him. She simply watched in silence.

Silence might mean she approved, at least to some degree.

Golden Ring thought it over carefully.

Samanto was right. Golden Ring had already started thinking of himself as part of the Sea Without Light. He was a player, and he'd confirmed that the Sea Without Light had been founded by players. But it was obviously recruiting more than just players.

Golden Ring didn't want his future teammate to be a mole who would stab him in the back.

"I think we need a second round of introductions," Golden Ring said. "We'll be working together, after all… We ought to know what our teammates can do, right?"

Wei Li laughed, and to everyone's surprise she was the first to agree. "I'm in."

"If you're all sharing, so will I," Samanto said offhandedly.

"Fine," Lancet said, and nothing more.

"My ability has to do with explosions. It's B-rank." Golden Ring was careful to stay vague about it.

"Mine is a mental-type supernatural ability. C-rank," said Wei Li.

Samanto said, "Long-range offensive type. C-rank."

Lancet went last. "Physical enhancement type. C-rank."

Wei Li's face changed slightly when she heard their abilities, but she quickly hid it.

As the only B-rank, Golden Ring stood head and shoulders above the other three and was instantly the strongest of the group.

Then Golden Ring heard a crisp tapping. Fer-de-lance was tapping the table lightly with her silver-gray mechanical hand. "You've talked plenty. Have you decided who it is?"

One by one, the four of them nodded.

Lancet turned his head slightly and asked in a low voice, "Fer-de-lance, you already had someone in mind. That's why you asked us. Our answers don't really matter, do they?"

Fer-de-lance gave the same answer as before. "You can think of it that way."

"You may now say who you suspect," she said.

Lancet's expression shifted a little. Very quietly, he said, "I abstain."

Golden Ring frowned. Lancet's move made him doubt the answer he'd settled on.

"I pick Golden Ring," Samanto said firmly. "The kid's way too eager. I think he's covering something up."

Wei Li didn't hold back. "I have two picks. Samanto and Lancet."

Golden Ring said, "Between Samanto and Lancet… I suspect Samanto more."

Samanto's eyes darkened. "Careful throwing around accusations you can't back up…"

Wei Li gave a cold laugh. Her gaze moved between Samanto and Lancet, and she spat out three words. "A pair of liars!"

"Mm." Fer-de-lance spoke calmly, without hurry. "Seize Samanto."

Samanto's face changed completely, and his calm act collapsed. He sprang up from the conference table and clenched his fist at the same time, hastily forming a transparent air bullet. It took shape in midair and shot out, aimed straight at Kui Xin.

All four of them had been disarmed when they arrived. With no firearms, they could only fight with their bodies and supernatural abilities.

Fer-de-lance, in charge of the room, was clearly the strongest, so Samanto attacked her first. He only wanted to slow her down, though. The moment he fired, he was already turning to make a break for it.

In an instant, Su Rong's shadow double stretched into a curtain that covered Kui Xin and blocked the air bullet. The pitch-black curtain wobbled violently like jelly as it absorbed the force, and it very nearly tore.

Wei Li grabbed a chair and swung it hard at Samanto. Right on cue, Amber's eyes lit up with a glowing blue light. Samanto's mind went hazy for a second, and the chair hit him square on and knocked him to the floor.

Lancet, who had abstained, didn't hesitate one bit this time. He lunged and knocked Samanto out with a single punch. He hit so hard that he cracked Samanto's jaw, and everyone heard the crisp snap of bone.

Samanto passed out.

Golden Ring had been on his feet for a while, but he hadn't managed to join the fight.

"Sorry about that. My ability's hard to control. If I use it, I'll blow up the whole room. Thanks for handling it, and sorry I wasn't any help." Golden Ring sat back down awkwardly.

"Amber," Kui Xin said. "Take him away."

As she spoke, she pulled a tranquilizer out of the gear bag she carried and signaled Amber to inject him.

Amber got up right away. He gave Samanto the sedative, hoisted him over his shoulder and walked into the spatial vortex Kui Xin had opened, disappearing from sight.

"There. The unstable element has been removed," Kui Xin said.

"But…" Wei Li's gaze settled on Lancet.

"I know what I'm doing," Kui Xin said.

Wei Li looked away. "Yes. Understood."

Golden Ring glanced at Lancet a couple of times, then looked away too, a thoughtful look on his face.

"You've passed the interview," Kui Xin said. "The Sea Without Light's standard for members is very simple. There are only two points. One, you have to be able to get things done. Two, you have to stay loyal to the organization. Supernatural abilities aren't the most important measure. Who you are as a person matters far more. As of today, you're members of the Sea Without Light."

At last… it was settled. Even though he'd known all along how it would turn out, Golden Ring still breathed a sigh of relief and smiled without meaning to.

Kui Xin raised her hand and opened another spatial vortex. "Wei Li, Golden Ring, you two leave first and stand by. Ash, go with them."

"Okay." Golden Ring stood up.

Wei Li had no objection and got up as well.

Su Rong's shadow double followed the two of them into the spatial vortex.

Once the vortex closed, only Kui Xin and Lancet were left in the meeting room.

Lancet's gaze was steady. He showed no sign of panic.

"How did Wei Li figure out there was something off about me and Samanto? Her mental ability..." Lancet's voice was low.

Wei Li's ability had a rank limit: her mind reading didn't work on supernatural ability users of a higher rank than her.

The moment Samanto and Lancet said they were C-rank, Wei Li had noticed something was wrong. If they really were only C-rank, why didn't her ability work on either of them? Golden Ring had been honest. He was B-rank, so it was perfectly normal that she couldn't hear his inner voice. That meant Lancet and Samanto had to be lying, and their abilities were above C-rank.

"Both of you hid your ability rank without even talking it over," Kui Xin said slowly. "What's that supposed to be? Some unspoken understanding between people with something to hide? If you want to play the pig to eat the tiger, you'd better make sure you can pass for a pig first. Your acts were terrible. You were seen through the second you walked in."

Any trick involving supernatural abilities had a hard time getting past Kui Xin's Probing Eye.

"I wasn't trying to play the pig to eat the tiger... No, okay, I was trying to play the pig, but I never meant to eat any tiger," Lancet said. "Sorry I held a few things back. I did it to protect myself, not for any other reason."

Lancet had claimed his ability was Rapid Healing... but it had never occurred to him that this was a trick Kui Xin had already worn out herself. After she'd gained Flesh Regeneration, she had also claimed her ability was Hyper-Speed Healing so she could hide that she had the ability to deprive.

"What's your number?" Kui Xin tapped the table with her metal fingers.

"Depriver, No. 5116," Lancet said.

Kui Xin asked another question. "Why did you abstain just now?"

"I was afraid the 'traitor-in-waiting' you mentioned meant me, since I'm a Depriver. Like you said, I've got something to hide. In that situation, pointing at someone else as the traitor would've looked like I was desperately trying to clear my own name," Lancet said. "You know the reputation Deprivators have on the forum. It's pretty ugly. I was worried the Sea Without Light would be more inclined to get rid of Deprivators."

"You were afraid we'd get rid of you, and you came anyway," Kui Xin said.

"There's a rumor that Depriver No. 233 is working with Black Snake. Of course, it's only a rumor. Nobody's actually proven anything," Lancet said. "I was pretty worried about how the Sea Without Light feels about Deprivators, but I decided to come anyway. Call it trying my luck."

"That's some gamble," Kui Xin said. "If your luck had gone the wrong way, you'd be dead."

"So far, it looks like my luck went the right way," Lancet said.

"Like I said, we only have two standards for taking people on. If you can meet both of them, it doesn't matter whether you're a Depriver or not," Kui Xin said. "Tell me what abilities you have."

"I killed a Blood-Variant and got Flesh Regeneration. Later I lucked into a Face-Changing ability," Lancet said. "The Face-Changing ability is B-rank. Pure dumb luck. Other than that, I have an E-rank Skin Hardening, but it's barely worth mentioning. It can't even stop a knife... I can hold my own in a fight, and I'm very comfortable with firearms..."

"What was it you do for work in the real world again?" Kui Xin made a point of asking, because Lancet had joined Black Snake's personal forum and had sent her his personal information.

Lancet hesitated. "I run a small company. I built it myself from nothing. I started my own business right after I graduated from university..."

Kui Xin looked straight at him. "Lancet... our organization needs talent like you!"
