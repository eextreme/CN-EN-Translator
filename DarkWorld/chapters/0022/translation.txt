She had to breathe lightly and step lightly, and she had to stay fast while doing it, or the enemy would overtake her.

It was a short distance, but it felt endless.

In the darkness and silence, all of Kui Xin's senses were heightened. She could hear her own heartbeat and her slow, even breathing, and she could faintly hear vibrations coming up through several floors.

She didn't dare talk to Adam freely anymore, afraid her voice would give her position away. Adam marked the escape route and the security officers' positions with red dots on the 3D map, and every so often it spoke up to steer Kui Xin around the search teams.

When Kui Xin reached the 29th floor, she heard Adam say, "The security officers have reached the eighth floor. They found Augus's body and confirmed that was where you were hiding earlier. They've been ordered to speed up the search, and Zhong Xinou has sent another fifteen-man squad into the building."

Now only a door stood between Kui Xin and the roof. It was an ordinary push door that would open with one shove.

But above the roof on the other side, hover police cars were floating, sweeping their searchlights back and forth.

Kui Xin looked down to check herself and confirmed that the optical camouflage was still working. Then she checked her mechanical arm. The explosive rounds had been reloaded, and the purely mechanical grappling claw still worked. She also had plenty of other firearms and ammunition on her. The fight with Augus had only used up some hand-cannon rounds and a few bombs, and almost nothing else was gone.

"Amber," Kui Xin said.

"I'm ready. I can move whenever you say," Amber answered.

Out of Kui Xin's sight, night-flying birds had settled along the edges of the high-rises, and some had landed on the levitation rails. They sat in neat rows like soldiers waiting for orders. It was a deeply eerie sight, but everyone on the scene was focused on finding a living person, and nobody noticed anything odd about the birds.

Kui Xin heard heavy footsteps downstairs. The security officers were closing in.

"Do it." Kui Xin rested her fingers on the door.

"Yes," Amber said.

The moment he spoke, the perched birds shot into the sky like bullets leaving a barrel, whoosh, whoosh, whoosh, and slammed head-on into the hover police cars overhead. A few even flew straight into a car's exhaust pipes and plugged them up completely.

"Bird strike!" the security officer in the driver's seat cried in surprise, fighting to steady the car and keep it from spinning out of control.

"Don't panic. We're flying at low speed, so a few little animals can't do much damage," the officer in the passenger seat said. "Did you forget to turn on the low-frequency noise deterrent?"

To prevent bird strikes and collisions, hover police cars usually ran low-frequency noise when they took to the air. Birds and bats found the sound unbearable and gave helicopters and hover cars a wide berth.

"It's on!" the driver said. "How could I make such a rookie mis—"

Before he could finish, more birds smacked into the windshield, smearing it with blood and feathers.

The next moment the warning lights inside the car came on. The engine droned, then started wheezing and sputtering like an old man's cough, as if it was about to die.

Adam's announcement came right on time: "Engine failure. Activating backup circuit system... Insufficient time for system reboot. Recommend parachute evacuation..."

As soon as Adam finished, the hover police car's engine cut out. The nose tipped down, and the whole car started to fall!

The two security officers inside screamed and hit the eject button. The roof split open, and both officers were launched out, seats and all.

Fwoosh!

Two little white flowers bloomed in the night sky. Strapped into their safety seats, the two security officers drifted down under their parachutes, drenched in cold sweat.

Boom!

The falling hover police car hit the edge of a building first, then tumbled down and crashed into the ground. Flames burst out and swallowed the wreck.

"Incident in the office building district. Requesting backup."

"Find out what caused the accident. Stay alert."

"Watch out for birds. I repeat, watch out for birds... These birds aren't behaving normally. This might not be an accident."

Reports kept pouring in over the Investigation Department's internal comms channel.

It had all happened so suddenly that no one noticed, while the birds were attacking the police cars, a transparent human figure slipping out of the stairwell exit on the roof of the building below. The door opened quietly and closed just as quietly, all in less than a second.

By the time the searchlight of the replacement patrol car swept over the roof again, she was already hanging from a window ledge on the outside of the building.

Kui Xin was sweating bullets. Her fingers were hooked over the top edge of the window frame, and the tips of her toes rested carefully on the bottom ledge as she clung to the side of the building, swaying. She glanced down. Good grief. It was more than twenty stories, and one look was enough to make her dizzy.

Not far off on the ground was a checkpoint, packed with barricades and heavily guarded. In the sky, hover police cars cast huge pools of light from their searchlights, and the pools drifted between the towers.

Ten meters below her ran a levitation rail, which was also ten meters out from the building's wall. It was a bit far, but she could reach it with the grappling claw. She just had to time it right.

Once she was on the rail, if a searchlight swung her way, she could hang underneath and hide in its shadow. When the light passed, she could run along the rail and get out of the area covered by the Void Domain as fast as possible.

Kui Xin slowly worked her way down from ledge to ledge. After she had climbed down two floors, she picked her moment, raised her left arm at the rail, and fired the grappling claw. With a whoosh, it wrapped around the rail's steel framework.

She retracted the claw's chain, and it swung her up onto the levitation rail. She hopped lightly on top, unhooked the claw, and let out a breath.

Step one, escape the building: done.

Step two, get onto the levitation rail: done.

Next came step three: sneak away along the rail.

Floating Port City's transit system ran twenty-four hours a day, but the battle downtown had damaged the rails, and every maglev tram in the city had been halted. Kui Xin didn't have to worry about getting flattened by an oncoming tram halfway along.

The pools of searchlight came and went with almost no pattern. All Kui Xin could rely on was her own speed and agility.

"Amber, coordinate carefully," Kui Xin said gravely.

"I will," Amber said.

"Adam." Kui Xin switched channels. "You..."

"I'll warn you in time about where the searchlights are pointing and how the hover cars are moving," Adam said, finishing her sentence.

Kui Xin went quiet. She tilted her head back to check where the hover police cars were and matched them against the red dots on the 3D map in her eyepiece. Then she took a deep breath, crouched low, and sprinted along the rail as fast as she could.

The instant the searchlight's beam slid off the rail, Kui Xin darted past, skimming its edge.

She had barely gone two steps when Adam said, "Front left!"

Without a moment's hesitation, Kui Xin locked her fingers around the edge of the levitation rail and dropped to hang from it. She used her momentum to swing slightly and caught the rail with her other hand too. Then she pulled herself up with both arms until her body lay parallel to the rail, hidden on its underside.

The pool of light drifted over Kui Xin and passed on without stopping.

Only after a few seconds did Kui Xin let her body hang down again. She let go with one hand, climbed back onto the rail, and kept going.

Drones flew past her one after another, but Adam filtered the data, so their scans showed nothing that mattered. The drones looked right past Kui Xin, and she made it a long way down the rail without trouble.

The tightest security was around the building where Augus's body had been found. A little farther on, she would be out of the core of the cordon, and the rest of the way would be much easier.

Kui Xin's frayed nerves eased a little. She crept forward step by step, slipping past the searchlights with practiced ease, and her mind grew steadily calmer.

Then, all of a sudden, someone on the Investigation Department's internal channel, which she'd been monitoring the whole time, said, "What's that? A person?"

Kui Xin's heart lurched, and a huge sense of danger crashed over her. Her instincts told her to spin around, and when she did, she saw a ghostly shadow floating in the air behind her!

The figure's outline was blurry, and it flickered as if it were unstable, like a ghost floating in the air, a specter watching her from behind layer after layer of curtains.

He had appeared without a sound, right in front of every security officer. What chilled Kui Xin was that his gaze seemed to pierce straight through her optical camouflage. He was looking at her!

He was... He was Angel!

"I've heard so much about you. I've wanted to meet you for a long time, Deprivator No. 233," Angel said. "Tonight you should be basking in victory. Killing No. 777, a fellow Depriver, must be well worth celebrating for you... I'm truly sorry to ruin the moment. But you're far too great a threat to us, so I'm afraid I'll have to ask you to die."

With that last line, the blurry figure dissolved. But the security officers who had seen his mental projection were already swinging their searchlights over! Angel showing himself had clearly been meant to point the way!

The light fell across Kui Xin's lower body and threw a shadow. Her position was exposed!

"Get out of there!" Adam's mechanical voice had never sounded so urgent.

Before Adam even spoke, Kui Xin had stepped back and rolled, dodging a spray of machine-gun fire. Black gun barrels had slid out from the sides of the hover police cars, and every hover car in the sky had its guns trained on her.

She had nowhere to retreat to. A jump from this height would kill her.

With her life on the line, Kui Xin caught the edge of the rail with one hand. Then, as if crossing monkey bars, she gripped the rail with both hands and swung herself along hand over hand, like a primate leaping and twisting through the branches of a dense jungle.

Sparks flew, and the clanging of bullets on metal went on and on.

Just then the birds arrived. One after another they flew fearlessly into the windshields and crammed themselves into the exhaust vents. Some hover cars stalled and dropped out of the sky, and some officers triggered their safety systems and ejected, escaping the falling police cars under parachutes.

Explosions went off one after another as the cars that had hit the ground blew up, and smoke spread everywhere. The machine guns fell silent for the moment, because security officers from several cars were still drifting down on their parachutes. Their descent paths crossed some of the firing lines, and the others didn't want to hit their own comrades, so they had to hold their fire.

Even that wasn't the worst of it. The worst of it was that Zhong Xinou and Harriman had been alerted and were on their way!

This time Kui Xin really was breaking out in a cold sweat. Her arms ached. She pulled herself back up onto the rail and hurried to change position while the guns were quiet.

But where could she even run? The rail was the only way!

Her mechanical left arm had lost a finger to a bullet, though that wouldn't really hurt her in a fight.

"Are you still alive? Kui Xin?" Amber sounded a little anxious. His birds still couldn't see her, so he didn't know whether she was dead or safe.

"Not dead yet," Kui Xin said quickly. "Angel showed up! He knows where we are!"

For the first time in a long while, she felt panic. If Angel could pop up whenever he liked while she ran for her life and point her out to the Special Intelligence Division and the Investigation Department, wasn't she going to be finished sooner or later?!

Kui Xin was trying to think of a way out alive, and Adam was trying to think of a way to get her out safely.

In the brief silence, Kui Xin heard Amber's heavy breathing over the comm channel.

"I'll... try to draw their attention. I'll pull the hover police cars' fire and lure Zhong Xinou and Harriman my way," Amber decided. "I don't know if my plan will work, or how much of the pressure I can take off you... Anyway, I'll do what I can. Run with everything you've got, Kui Xin."

As he said this, he was standing on a distant signal tower. It was two hundred meters tall, and from up there he had a clear view of everything far below.

He unslung the K80 sniper rifle from his back, put his eye to the scope, and rested his finger on the trigger.

Angel's shadow suddenly appeared in front of him and blocked the eyepiece.

The ghostly image flickered in and out, and so did his voice.

Angel's mental ability was getting a stronger and stronger hold on them. Was it because Floating Port City was too close to his real body? Amber knew that some mental abilities were affected by distance.

"Your choice surprises me a little, Amber," Angel said. "Between living and sacrificing yourself, you'd actually pick sacrifice? You've betrayed us twice in a row. I thought you were the kind of person who put yourself and your brother first... Or is it that you're not choosing sacrifice, you're choosing that person? No... You're not choosing sacrifice. You're pledging your loyalty to that person, aren't you?"

No. Of course not.

Amber's lips moved slightly. He stared at Angel's phantom, and a single word slipped out between his teeth.

"Scram!"

The shackles on his mind broke open. He pushed past his limit and drove the phantom away. A glowing blue light nearly filled his pupils, and in that moment his B-class supernatural ability rose to A-class.

Amber pulled the trigger. The bullet left the barrel in a burst of flame, crossed several hundred meters, and struck Zhong Xinou's helmet dead on!

"—CLANG!"

The impact knocked Zhong Xinou backward, and his helmet now had a clear dent in it!

The attack wasn't over. Several more bullets quickly hit Zhong Xinou and Harriman, who stood beside him.

Harriman dragged Zhong Xinou behind a wall. His subordinates stepped in front of them, and the security officers moved up as well.

"Calculate the trajectory!" Zhong Xinou reacted instantly.

"Trajectory calculated. The sniper is on the top level of the signal tower in Zone C," Adam reported promptly.

Amber put his rifle away. He kicked aside the spent casings scattered across the floor and left the signal tower without wasting a second.

His face was calm. As he walked, he reloaded the guns he carried, getting ready for the fierce battle ahead.
