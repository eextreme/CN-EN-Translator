"A third of the security officers have been split off, along with one of the helicopters carrying heavy weapons. But Harriman is part of the force hunting Amber. She and Zhong Xinou conferred briefly and then split up," Adam reported. "They don't know how many people Amber has with him, and they don't know how much fight you have left in you, so they're being cautious."

Only a third of the security officers had left, but Harriman alone was more than enough to make up for the missing firepower. Her ability couldn't be used anywhere near Zhong Xinou, so splitting up was the only way to get the most out of the two of them. Still, with a good number of officers reassigned and the heavily armed helicopter gone too, the pressure on Kui Xin had eased a little.

But she could already guess how a fight between Amber and Harriman would end. His ability leaned toward support and control and had very little offensive power. All of his fighting strength came from himself, not his ability.

One blast of Harriman's Annihilation energy would be enough to blow Amber to ashes.

Kui Xin took one deep breath after another, trying to calm her pounding heart. Her blood was racing, adrenaline surged through her, her body temperature climbed, and sweat broke out on her forehead and palms.

She ran a hand over her mechanical arm. Some parts were missing from its fingers, and the tip of the index finger was leaking current. Wires were exposed and sparks crackled and snapped. In this state it could no longer transform into a hand cannon, but the grappling claw ran purely on mechanical power and still more or less worked. Its internal parts hadn't been damaged.

"Can you shut off the driving permissions on a hover police car?" Kui Xin asked, talking very fast. "If I hijack one, can I force my way into the driving permissions? And if you give me the permissions, will that expose you?"

She hadn't been at the Investigation Department long enough to go through driver training, and she knew very little about how hover cars were built.

"Yes. Yes. There's some risk, but not necessarily," Adam answered just as quickly. "Hover cars are semi-automated, and every system can be operated manually. We just blame the irregular permission changes on hackers like we did before. The Federation doesn't know who we have on our side anyway."

There were two reasonable ways to explain why the search drones had flown right past Kui Xin without raising an alarm. One, the drones had been taken over by outside hackers who were filtering the data they sent back. Two, the fugitive was carrying some kind of special jamming device.

Adam would have to play it by ear.

"Can you make the driver's seat and the passenger seat eject automatically?" Kui Xin asked her last question.

"Yes," Adam said.

Kui Xin pressed her lips together. "Then I'll need you to work with me on this, Adam."

Searchlights slid along the rail. After a brief spell of chaos, a small group of security officers went after Amber while the rest re-formed their ranks and resumed an orderly search.

On the 3D map in Kui Xin's eyepiece, the red dot representing Zhong Xinou was the biggest and brightest, and it was moving toward her.

She had quietly made it almost a hundred meters in a straight line, but then Angel had given away her hiding spot. Zhong Xinou knew exactly how his ability worked. He knew he couldn't let Kui Xin break out of its range, so he had moved himself to bring her inside the Void Domain.

Luckily, Amber's trick of crashing birds into the vehicles had left everyone there badly shaken. A bird strike on a hover car or an aircraft at high speed could be catastrophic, so nobody dared to fly fast. Even Zhong Xinou wouldn't risk taking a helicopter.

Even so, there was no way Kui Xin could outpace Zhong Xinou by sneaking along the rail on foot.

And that was without even counting Angel, who was lurking somewhere in the shadows, ready to pop out at any moment and point her out...

She couldn't let this drag on. Kui Xin had to break out of the Void Domain as fast as possible and then use her spatial vortex to teleport. That way, even with Angel guiding him, it would take Zhong Xinou some time to find her, and with that time she could even get to another city.

*Boom...*

This time the explosion didn't come from anywhere near Kui Xin. It came through her earpiece.

Labored breathing sounded in a steady rhythm, grating on her eardrums. She heard Amber cough. He seemed to be hurt.

Harriman and the security officers had already reached him... Though it was more likely that Amber had given away his position on purpose to draw them after him.

"Hold on a few more minutes, Amber," Kui Xin said under her breath.

She tilted her head back, her blue mechanical eye scanning the sky for a target, weighing which car would be the easiest to take.

The hover police cars in the air usually carried only two officers each and were armed with mounted machine guns. Most of the security officers were down on the ground. If she took out the two in a car, the car was hers.

She stopped for the moment, searching for a target while staying on guard.

She had a feeling Angel would show up again soon. The last round of gunfire hadn't killed her, and he was dead set on seeing her dead.

She waited quietly for two or three seconds, and sure enough, her instincts sounded a warning.

This time Angel's phantom was even closer. It appeared practically pressed against her body.

He said softly, "Did you really think you could get away?"

Kui Xin pushed off with her legs and leapt clear.

*Clang clang clang...* The sound of bullets hitting metal set her nerves on edge. The bullets passed straight through Angel's phantom. He smiled and faded away, and the next second he reappeared behind her.

"Your struggling is doomed to fail," he said again.

Machine-gun fire raked toward her! Just like last time, Kui Xin dropped off the rail and grabbed its edge with one hand, hanging underneath. Hidden by her optical camouflage, she was only briefly caught in the hail of bullets before it swept on down the rail.

Kui Xin seized her chance and sprang back up, sprinting along the rail as fast as she could, straight toward where the bullets were coming from.

The grappling claw's chain reached twenty meters. The high-strength alloy wire was ductile and flexible, but it wasn't long enough! Her mechanical eye measured the straight-line distance from the rail to the hover police car precisely: a full twenty-five meters!

Even if Kui Xin got close to the car, the grappling claw still couldn't reach it!

How was she supposed to cross those last five meters?

Her tactical boots pounded the steel rail, striking sparks. A security officer noticed something was off and swung his gun around.

Just as the hail of bullets was about to engulf her, Kui Xin bent her knees slightly and drove off the soles of her feet, putting everything into it. She launched herself high off the maglev track, toward the hover police car!

She had exactly one chance. If the grappling claw missed, she would fall out of the sky and hit the ground as a smear of meat.

The outline of the hover police car was reflected clearly in both her blue mechanical eye and her dark brown natural one. That second seemed to stretch on forever. She soared into the sky like a bird, light and powerful, except that unlike a bird, she had no wings.

But she did have a mechanical limb that could reach out for her: her mechanical left arm.

*Whoosh!*

The grappling claw shot out, and its chain streamed silver light beneath the sky.

Kui Xin cleared those five meters, as impassable as a chasm, and the chained grappling claw carried her across the remaining twenty.

*Clang!*

The grappling claw hooked the hover police car's tail fin dead on! The car rocked in midair at the sudden pull.

The chain whirred back in, and Kui Xin hung off the back of the car for a moment before climbing up onto it.

At that same moment, the roof of the car split open, revealing two panicked security officers inside.

Adam: "Driving system failure. Abandoning the vehicle is recommended... Data error... Data error... Abnormal driver status detected. Initiating emergency protocol. Automatic ejection activated."

"Adam, wait!" The two security officers in the driver's and passenger seats had barely gotten the words out in shock before the recoil pinned them to their seats.

They felt themselves go weightless as they shot high into the air. White parachutes bloomed, and before they even knew what was happening, they had been forcibly removed from the battlefield.

With no one at the controls, the hover police car began to drop. Kui Xin nimbly slipped inside, swung her leg over, and stomped on the accelerator. She grabbed the controls with both hands and wrenched the car onto a new heading, dodging the concentrated fire from the other security officers.

Thankfully the car's shell was bulletproof. Even if it took a few rounds, it would hold up for a while.

Since the seats were gone, she had to half-squat while turning the steering wheel and flooring the accelerator, a twisted posture with more than a touch of the ridiculous to it.

Adam asked a crucial question. "Do you know how to drive?"

Kui Xin floored the accelerator and roared in exasperation, "No idea!"

At the Investigation Department, their team leader Shu Xuyao had always been the one driving. In the First World, Kui Xin hadn't gotten around to taking her driving test yet, and the vehicles she was most comfortable with were electric scooters and motorbikes. The Second World Kui Xin had learned to drive at Mechanical Dawn, but hover police cars were built completely differently from regular cars!

Her driving knowledge might be thin, but there was one thing Kui Xin did know: flying too high made you an easy target.

Her overheated brain cooled down. Remembering how Shu Xuyao had handled the controls, she brought the car down to the ground, then stomped on the accelerator and drifted, swinging the car a hundred and eighty degrees before charging forward.

The hover police car was built tough, from incredibly sturdy materials. Kui Xin floored the accelerator and the engine roared. Blue flames spewed from the exhaust, blazing like sharp lightsabers! The car shot forward like an arrow loosed from a bow, smashing everything in its path, and burst straight through the roadblock ahead!

The security officers guarding the roadblock threw themselves to either side of the road to avoid being hit.

Kui Xin yanked the steering wheel hard. The wheels screeched against the ground, leaving a long black skid mark on the road, and the car slid through the curve before quickly straightening out.

She drove that car the way a general rides a warhorse. The engine roared as it tore through every obstacle, and nothing seemed able to stand in her way.

The car wove between the high-rises, and she used the buildings to block her enemies' line of sight and stay out of their fire.

The 3D map updated in real time.

Adam warned her, "Two convoys are flanking you on the roads to your left and right, and there are hover police cars overhead, though they're keeping their distance because they're afraid of bird strikes. You've also got pursuers behind you. A helicopter has taken off, and its weapons could easily do damage to your car. Zhong Xinou is with the convoy on your right. There's just one building between you, and the gap is only sixty meters right now."

Kui Xin hit a button on the console, and the car switched from road mode to flight mode. It lifted into the air and spun another hundred and eighty degrees. She had turned around and was heading back the way she came!

Zhong Xinou didn't see it coming. Kui Xin was in a single car with the pursuing convoys still some way off, so switching to flight mode and turning around was easy. Zhong Xinou, on the other hand, had several cars with him, and getting all of them turned around at once was bound to be chaotic. By the time they had reoriented, Kui Xin had left them more than a hundred meters behind.

She turned onto another road just in time to slip past the pursuers directly behind her, and the road ahead was clear.

Two hundred forty-five meters... two hundred sixty-five meters... Kui Xin kept an eye on the distance shown on her eyepiece, counting it off in her head.

The cut on her face still stung faintly and showed no sign of healing.

She had to get farther away!

The helicopter's rotors were hard to make out over the roar of the car's engine, but it was definitely getting closer.

"The helicopter is about to fire its heavy ordnance," Adam said. "Those are miniature homing missiles!"

"Shit!" Kui Xin swore and took the car higher.

The display on the car's console worked as a rearview mirror. On it, Kui Xin clearly saw a burst of golden-red "fireworks" bloom from the gunship's launcher. The compact missile, shaped like a swordfish cruising through the sea, chased its prey at tremendous speed.

Kui Xin's face was grim. She didn't turn the wheel. She just floored the accelerator and drove straight into the dark skyscraper ahead!

There was a thunderous crash and glass rained down. The front of Kui Xin's car crumpled and the windshield shattered as she plowed into the skyscraper. Kui Xin fought to keep herself steady as the car shook violently. Her optical camouflage button was knocked loose, but she had no time to deal with it.

Another crash followed as more glass burst apart, and Kui Xin came hurtling back out of the skyscraper!

Barely two or three seconds after she shot out, a dazzling fireball erupted behind her. The missile had struck something solid inside the skyscraper and detonated, and the shockwave shoved the car forward.

At that hour of the early morning, most businesses had shut down for the night, and the Investigation Department had cleared the pedestrians and traffic from the roads. Otherwise, no amount of money could have covered the damage from this incident.

Three hundred fifty-five meters! Kui Xin dropped lower and wove between the buildings to shake the helicopter.

It didn't matter if the helicopter stayed on her tail. As long as she could open up distance from Zhong Xinou, she won.

"Engine overload! Engine overload!" an alarm blared from the console.

Kui Xin ignored it and kept the accelerator floored.

The car's cooling system was running at maximum, but the alarms kept going.

Three hundred eighty meters!

The helicopter sped up and closed the gap, and its loaded launcher locked onto Kui Xin.

Just then, the birds that had been quiet for so long suddenly arrived. A huge flock slammed head-on into the helicopter's windshield in an explosion of feathers and blood. At their combined speed, the impact spiderwebbed the windshield. The instruments went haywire for a moment, and the missile launch was put on hold.

Four hundred meters!

Kui Xin gripped the wheel so hard that the bones and veins stood out on her right hand. She felt a fierce, numb itch spread through the wound on her cheek, and that discomfort was her trumpet call of victory.

Kui Xin had broken out of the Void Domain that erased every ability!

She didn't let her guard down. A blue portal opened in the night sky, larger than any spatial vortex she'd made before. Kui Xin stared straight ahead and drove the car, with herself in it, headfirst into the vortex.

The black storm seemed capable of obliterating everything, and the energy gathered at Harriman's fingertips could bring death to anyone.

Amber was covered in blood. The Annihilation energy had only grazed one side of his upper body, but that side of his combat suit had disintegrated, and the arm beneath was a bloody mess, its skin peeling away horribly. Without the suit's protection, he had also been shot, and there was a ragged, bloody bullet hole in his shoulder blade.

Controlling the birds to attack the helicopter so Kui Xin could escape had used up the last of Amber's strength. He lay on the ground, about to die.

"Kui Xin..." His vision was blurring from blood loss. "You have to keep... your promise..."

Before the Annihilation energy hit him, he wanted to hear Kui Xin's answer.

He had waited less than a second, but to him it felt like forever. He had to get that promise. He had to!

The answer he was waiting for came.

A deep blue vortex opened in front of Amber. Lying on his back, he suddenly opened his eyes wide as that familiar blue filled his vision. It hit him like a shot of adrenaline, and his failing heart seemed to beat a little harder.

A car with a wrecked front end and a dented body dropped out of the vortex. Behind the shattered windshield was Kui Xin's calm face. She brought her war machine down between Amber and the danger.

The S-class Matter Reorganization she had acquired so recently activated! The molecular makeup of the car's metal flashed through Kui Xin's mind, ash appeared, and the hover police car beneath her began breaking down from the inside out!

The ash-gray matter swirled like leaping flames.

The Annihilation energy left Harriman's fingertips.

The pitch-black orb struck the hover police car Kui Xin was in. With a low hum, what was left of the car disintegrated completely, and at the same time the gray matter around Kui Xin's body reflected the concentrated ball of Annihilation energy back.

The cold menace hadn't fully left Harriman's face when shock and fury rose in her eyes. Her own returned attack knocked her back several steps.

Held up by the gray matter, Kui Xin landed smoothly. She took three steps back to Amber, crouched beside him, lifted him up, and slung him over her shoulder.

Amber's hands hung limp, and blood dripped steadily from his fingertips, staining Kui Xin's combat suit red.

She said, very softly, "Thank you."

Another spatial vortex opened. Ignoring Harriman, Kui Xin left the scene with Amber.

"You have to... keep your promise..." Amber's head rested on Kui Xin's shoulder as he kept murmuring stubbornly. "Save Obsidian."

"I will," Kui Xin said. "Right now we need to find a doctor to treat you."

"If I die, will you still keep your promise and save him?" Amber was barely conscious.

"I will." Kui Xin paused. "You're not dead yet, Amber."

At last Amber seemed reassured. He closed his eyes and passed out.
