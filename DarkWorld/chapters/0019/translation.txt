A supernatural ability that could act directly on matter itself: Matter Annihilation.

Kui Xin very nearly swore out loud.

The S-class Special Intelligence Division agent seemed to hate Augus deeply. His Matter Reorganization and Matter Annihilation belonged to the same category, so in theory, if one of them stripped the other's ability, the two could merge and upgrade.

Augus's Matter Reorganization had started out very low-level. He must have killed a lot of people in the Second World to upgrade it. Had he happened to kill a relative or friend of that S-class? Was he actually being hunted by an S-class Awakened?

Hurting others even when it gained him nothing was exactly the sort of thing Augus would do.

When he'd said he would "drag her down with him," he'd meant pulling Kui Xin into the fight so that her existence would be exposed for all to see.

Kui Xin clenched her teeth and whipped her head around to look at Augus, only to find the bastard smiling.

"They must have caught you on camera, Mai." Augus's smile was strange. He was so pleased with himself for having tricked Kui Xin that he even used her old name. "Now you're their enemy too. They'll know you're Deprivator No. 233."

He wanted to throw one more person into the chaos and make it even messier. He wanted to shove Kui Xin forward to draw the Special Intelligence Division's attention and use her to redirect their hatred. More than that, he wanted to see her suffer, to see his enemies suffer, because it gave him a sense of balance. See? Someone else was just as unlucky as he was.

"You seem to be able to teleport over long distances." Augus talked faster. "You can get out of this fight, but after today the Special Intelligence Division will have your name on file... The S-class Matter Annihilation is right there on that gunship. Don't you want it? If we team up, we'll have a shot..."

Augus was right.

What he didn't know was that the Special Intelligence Division had had Kui Xin on file for a long time. They just hadn't been able to connect the leader of the Sea Without Light in White Whale City with the woman in Floating Port City.

After today, there was no doubt Kui Xin would earn a very prominent spot on the Special Intelligence Division's wanted list. From then on, they would make her a top-priority kill-or-capture target, and an urgent one at that, to be dealt with by any means necessary.

Kui Xin shot Augus an icy, cutting glare. The gunship was drawing closer and closer. She needed to make a decision.

Her advantage was mobility. She could leave any fight at any moment. That powerful mobility was the very reason she had gone to any lengths to get the spatial vortex.

She had only chased Augus along the hover tracks tonight because she didn't want to let a once-in-a-lifetime chance slip by and let him escape. If she wanted to leave, she could leave at any time. Very little could stop her. Not even an S-class Matter Annihilation.

"That 'Matter Annihilation' is here for you." Kui Xin sneered. "In that case, there's no reason for me to get involved. Goodbye, Augus. I hope I get to see your corpse."

Having sincerely delivered her "blessing," she opened a spatial vortex behind her.

Augus's expression froze. He never would have imagined Kui Xin would bail on the fight so decisively.

Didn't she want his ability? Didn't she want the S-class Matter Annihilation? She had been scheming every way she could to kill him, and his taunts had successfully enraged her. Judging by everything she had done before, she clearly wasn't someone who would let others walk all over her... And yet she was just leaving?!

Under Augus's frozen gaze, Kui Xin stepped into the spatial vortex and gave him a mocking wave.

Only an idiot would stay.

Kui Xin's will to win wasn't currently strong enough to override her will to live. What did Augus's personal feud with "Annihilation" have to do with her? Better to let the dogs tear each other apart while she sat back and reaped the rewards.

Forcing her way into the fight would be the least sensible choice. Three S-classes brawling in the city center? That was a death wish.

She would leave, but she wouldn't go far.

The roar of the gunship's rotors rattled her eardrums. The blonde woman standing at the edge of the cabin looked down at Augus, raised her right hand and extended her index finger.

At the tip of her finger, a small black sphere that seemed to bend the light around it was taking shape, like a black hole that could draw in everything.

With a seemingly casual flick, she sent the black sphere shooting from her fingertip. It streaked a black line through the air and, moving almost too fast for the eye to follow, slammed with a boom into the section of hover track where Augus stood.

Augus leapt backward. The gray matter swirling around his body formed a sturdy shield that protected him so he wouldn't be hurt in the fall.

He had meant to use Shadow Realm to slip into the shadow space and avoid the attack, but the ability failed to activate.

The corner of Augus's eye twitched, and he realized in an instant: Deprivator No. 233 hadn't gone far!

Augus had been hit with Zero once before in the First World, and he knew that the restriction would wear off as long as he got far enough away from her. His abilities were still disabled, which meant she hadn't left the battlefield.

She wanted them to wear each other down, and then she would swoop in and claim the spoils.

As Augus fell, the black sphere that had hit the track suddenly expanded, swelling from the size of a peanut to tens of thousands of times larger, until it was a giant black ball more than twenty meters across. The hover track in midair was swallowed whole inside it.

Then the black ball dissipated, and the section of hover track it had engulfed had simply vanished. Where the track had been, there was now nothing at all. The rails, steel cables and wires had been annihilated by that terrifying power, their very existence erased.

Watching that horrifying sight, Augus felt, for just a split second, the urge to retreat.

He could easily do what Deprivator No. 233 had done: leave the fight and bide his time. He had already achieved part of his goal tonight, after all. Deprivator No. 233's existence couldn't be hidden anymore. He had successfully dragged her down with him.

Fighting an S-class Awakened whose mastery of her ability far surpassed his own would be extremely unwise, especially with his abilities still restricted by Zero...

Under the protection of Matter Reorganization, Augus landed safely, then immediately turned and ran for it!

His posture wasn't exactly panicked, but no matter how you looked at it, he was fleeing like a rat.

"Prepare to deploy the ground vehicle. Prepare to deploy the drone swarm." Harriman put on her helmet and went back into the cabin.

Besides the pilot, the cabin also held a specially modified police motorcycle.

She straddled the bike. The metal hatch in the floor of the cabin clanked open, and the motorcycle dropped straight out of the bottom of the gunship. As it fell, a paraglider shot out of the bike's frame and unfurled, softening the impact of the landing.

The moment the motorcycle touched down, the canopy lines attached to it unhooked themselves with a whoosh.

Harriman twisted the throttle. The tires ground against the road, kicking up billowing smoke, and with a piercing screech the bike shot down the street like an arrow loosed from a bow, chasing after Augus.

Like a swarm of bees following their queen, a dark mass of black drones flew down the street, tracking the target.

"Matter Annihilation. Her operational codename in the Special Intelligence Division is simply 'Annihilation.' Her real name is Kelly Harriman, and she's one of the few Awakened in the Special Intelligence Division whose supernatural ability data has been made public," Adam explained over Kui Xin's earpiece. "The reason it was made public is that her ability is extremely dangerous, so powerful it's uncontrollable. Even the teammates who carry out missions with her have to run far away when she lifts her restrictions. If they get caught in her indiscriminate attacks, their bodies will be completely annihilated too."

Kui Xin was hiding inside a skyscraper not far away, watching the fight through a one-way glass window.

She kept Zero active. Within this range, it could still affect Augus.

"That little black ball Harriman released..." Kui Xin asked cautiously.

"My guess is it's a way of releasing her ability that she developed herself, for long-range attacks," Adam said. "Otherwise, the moment she activated her ability, the gunship she was standing on would be disintegrated."

Kui Xin worked it out.

Augus's ability was well-rounded. It could not only dissolve matter but also reorganize it, making him something of an all-rounder with every stat filled out. Harriman's ability, on the other hand, had put every point into attack. Her offense was maxed out, which left her with weaknesses in other areas. She couldn't use her ability to support herself the way Augus did with Matter Reorganization, and if she didn't control it well, she would wallop her own teammates.

"This Ms. Harriman seems to have a very bad temper." Kui Xin had picked up a few things from the brief clash between her and Augus just now. "And she seems to prefer working alone. That might be because of her ability. She doesn't really need teammates. If anything, they'd hold her back. And she has plenty of confidence in herself..."

"You're right," Adam said. "I've heard the head of the Investigation Department in the Federal Administrative Center talk about her. Kelly Harriman has an extreme personality. She was born into a powerful family and became an S-class when she was barely past thirty. She's rarely faced setbacks, and people like that tend to be extremely arrogant. For example..."

Boom!

Augus raised a stone wall across the road to block Harriman, but with a twitch of her finger, a huge breach blew open in the middle of the wall, and the ground beneath it turned into a round crater as well.

She jumped her motorcycle right over the crater without so much as blinking.

Augus burst into a 24-hour convenience store by the road, gray debris flying through the air. The shopkeeper on duty inside fled in terror. Harriman didn't spare the poor man so much as a glance. A black sphere of energy condensed from Matter Annihilation shot forward, and half the convenience store faded into nothingness in a warped silence.

Harriman tore out of the store on her motorcycle like lightning, running over the rubble, and charged straight at Augus.

"That style... very Federation, very plutocrat..." Kui Xin was receiving the footage from the roadside cameras. "She really does come from a big family. That reckless, consequences-be-damned attitude is exactly like the Popov family's."

Kui Xin put her spectating on hold, opened a spatial vortex and stepped through, moving to coordinates a little closer to Augus and Harriman.

Augus was about to run out of the area covered by Zero, and once he did, his other abilities would come back into play.

She weighed the pros and cons calmly. Between Augus dying and getting her hands on his abilities, the first mattered more to her. There would be other abilities, but chances to finish off Augus didn't come along often.

Kui Xin could reach a tacit understanding with Harriman too, even though she was an S-class Awakened from a hostile faction. It would be an unspoken partnership: kill the man we both want dead.

She had tacit understandings with plenty of her enemies. Augus was the only one she wanted no understanding with at all. The idea made her want to throw up. Getting tangled up with him was as disgusting as getting shit stuck on you, and that was one of the reasons she'd refused to team up with Augus to kill Harriman.

"Amber, change position and stay out of sight," Kui Xin said over the comm channel. "They're heading southeast. Be ready to adjust your sniping spot at any time."

"Understood." Amber had already packed up his rifle and was moving.

The fight was changing from one second to the next, and how they attacked would have to depend on how things played out.

But just as he left the sports center with the rifle over his shoulder, a ghostly figure appeared in the middle of the empty, night-shrouded road. Its outline almost melted into the darkness.

The figure's face was a blur, yet Amber stopped dead in his tracks. He took a step back, his pupils dilating.

Never before had his face shown such a naked mix of shock and fear.

"You..." Amber stared at the figure, shaken and unsure, and forced out a single word. "Angel?"

"It's me, Amber," the figure said flatly. "I never expected you to switch sides again. What would they call you in the other world... a turncoat who's served three masters?"

Amber acted as if he hadn't heard the mockery in his words. "Do whatever you want. I don't care anymore."

"Is that so?" said Angel. "You seem a little more decisive than I imagined. A little braver."

With those words, the figure faded away.

"Amber." Kui Xin's voice came through his comm earpiece. "Who were you talking to just now? Angel?"

Cold sweat ran down Amber's temple. "It was him. He was talking to me through a mental projection, so you couldn't hear him. The Resistance knows we're here. He sensed us."
