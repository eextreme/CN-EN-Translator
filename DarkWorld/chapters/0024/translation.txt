In a filthy, run-down slum, Kui Xin and Amber found an inn that was reasonably clean and reasonably well hidden.

From the outside the little inn was falling apart, but the soundproofing inside was decent and it was fairly quiet. Kui Xin checked the room thoroughly, then sat down on the bed, exhausted.

She wanted nothing more than to collapse and sleep, and so did Amber, but circumstances didn't really allow it.

Given the peculiar nature of Angel's supernatural abilities, Kui Xin strongly suspected that wherever they stayed, they were about as hidden as a lighthouse. The only questions were whether the Resistance would suddenly show up, and when.

Both of them needed a deep sleep to give their brains a proper rest, but the reality was that the Resistance could come charging in at any moment. Even if they didn't, they could easily point the Investigation Department or the Special Intelligence Division their way.

The mental coordinates Angel had left in their heads were the product of a supernatural ability, so naturally a supernatural ability could remove them too. The catch was that Kui Xin and Amber's mental powers had to be stronger than Angel's.

"No, it didn't work." Amber had been trying for a long time with his eyes closed, and he gave up on rooting out the coordinates with a helpless sigh. "It's too stubborn. But I could feel its foundations shaking... If Obsidian and I worked together, we'd have a much better chance of getting rid of it."

"Are you and Obsidian the only artificial humans at Mechanical Dawn with mental supernatural abilities?" Kui Xin asked. "Are there any other artificial humans who survived?"

"Not as far as I know. The two of us are one of a kind," Amber said. "Mental supernatural abilities aren't like the others... A mind only comes into being once there's self-awareness, and mental abilities only grow out of a mind. The artificial humans of our model who never developed self-awareness are all brain-dead. Even if they grow up in a glass tank, they're just living vegetables. They can't even open their eyes."

"Brain-dead?" said Kui Xin. "That's really strange... So you two developing thoughts and feelings was a fluke that was bound to happen sooner or later."

"Our budding consciousness gave rise to a 'mind,' and we woke up in a kind of haze. That's the only reason we didn't end up as rejects," Amber said. "It's also one of the reasons Eve doesn't want to kill Obsidian. We're valuable test subjects. A non-renewable resource."

Kui Xin said, "Actually, every artificial human carries my genes. Mechanical Dawn spliced fragments of my genes into the artificial humans' genomes to raise the success rate. Kui Haidong told me so. My genes are a kind of master key. They're highly compatible and very adaptable, and they combine well with the genes of other supernatural ability users, keeping the abilities intact while raising the artificial humans' odds of survival."

"...Seriously?" It was the first time Amber had heard this, and an odd look crossed his face. "How similar are our genes?"

"Not blood-relative similar. You don't look anything like me," Kui Xin snorted. "Your face has got to be closer to your original's... They only edited in a few key gene fragments. We share some genetic similarity, sure, but nowhere near that much. My fragments probably make up a tiny fraction."

"My physiological age is eighteen, and I grew at a natural rate," Amber said. "If you do the math, they must have taken your genes when you were four or five?"

"Probably. Maybe even earlier, since gene fusion research takes time," Kui Xin said. "Kui Haidong probably got in touch with Eve not long after I was born."

"You identify with your identity in this world?" Amber had picked up on a few details in the way she spoke.

She had said "not long after I was born"... "I"?

Amber had keenly latched on to that curious little word.

"I don't like this world much, but the minds of my two selves from both worlds have already merged. No matter how much I don't want to admit it, the facts are the facts," Kui Xin said. "I've become one with the other 'Kui Xin.' I don't really know how far along the other players' merging is, but becoming one is only a matter of time."

She didn't explain any further, and instead asked a more detailed question. "How does the mental resonance between you and Obsidian work? Can you tell me?"

"Mental synchronization. Because we have the same genes and the same bodily structure, our minds can stay in sync," Amber said.

"Then... is there any chance we could do mental synchronization?" Kui Xin reasoned. "I have mental supernatural abilities too, and some of your gene fragments came from me."

Amber gave it serious thought. "Not very likely. Our bodies and brain structures are too different, and even though our abilities fall in the same category, different abilities have different effects. Still, we could give it a try. It's not like trying would hurt."

Kui Xin nodded. "Then let's try... Give me a minute."

She called up the game system, and the skill panel appeared.

The skill list stretched on so far she couldn't see the bottom... Killing Depriver 777 really had earned her a lot of new skill books.

Her S-rank abilities were Spatial Vortex and Matter Reorganization.

Her A-rank abilities were Soul-Bewitching Voice, Mental Command, Air Deflection, Death Cycle, Flesh Regeneration, Data Control, Blood Spirit and Zero. After absorbing Augus's Flesh Regeneration, Kui Xin's own Flesh Regeneration had automatically risen to A-rank. Before, hers couldn't regrow severed limbs, but after the upgrade her lost arm, her eye and her skull had all grown back.

Soul-Bewitching Voice and Mental Command were marked as fusible.

Her B-rank abilities were Iron Bone, Probing Eye, Shadow Realm, Blood Thorn, Threat Marking, Electromagnetic Detection, Blazing Blade, Mental Immunity, Damage Sync... Threat Marking and Probing Eye were both detection abilities that worked through the naked eye, and they were marked as fusible.

Her C-rank abilities were Shadow Walk, Blood Burn, Trajectory Lock, Serpent Body and Magnetic Sword.

At D-rank she had Telekinetic Control.

At E-rank she had Status Perception.

She only needed one of Threat Marking and Probing Eye. Kui Xin chose to keep Probing Eye, and when she fused Threat Marking into it, a prompt sounded in her head.

"[Probing Eye · A-rank]: Your eyes can see exactly what your opponent's supernatural abilities do..."

Kui Xin knew this description well enough, so she skipped the voice prompt and kept reading.

Soul-Bewitching Voice, Mental Command and Mental Immunity were all mental abilities. Mental Immunity made her immune to mind control. If she raised its rank high enough, could it uproot or block Angel's mental coordinates?

After weighing it for a moment, Kui Xin chose to fuse Soul-Bewitching Voice into Mental Immunity.

Soul-Bewitching Voice was a flawed ability. It needed sound as a medium, and it had to be her real voice, which was very inconvenient for Kui Xin. By comparison, the Mental Command she had taken from Augus was far more useful. It needed no medium and could issue orders straight into an enemy's brain. She could only keep one of the two, so Kui Xin kept Mental Command and gave up Soul-Bewitching Voice to upgrade Mental Immunity.

"[Mental Immunity · A-rank]: You have extremely strong resistance to every type of mental ability. Your brain is wrapped in walls of bronze and iron."

The jump from A-rank to S-rank seemed to take an enormous amount of experience. Even fusing the A-rank Soul-Bewitching Voice into the B-rank Mental Immunity hadn't been enough to push it to S-rank. Abilities of the same rank weren't all equal either. These two were probably on the weaker end of their rank to begin with.

Shadow Walk and Shadow Realm could also be fused... Kui Xin faced a tough choice.

"[Shadow Realm · B-rank]: You can hide your body and the items on you inside shadow space. Shadows are the medium that connects you to shadow space. Without a shadow as a medium, you cannot reach shadow space. If the shadow medium disappears, your body will be forcibly ejected from shadow space."

All in all, it was the same type of ability as Shadow Walk, a hybrid of shadow-type and space-type abilities. Both needed shadows as a medium or a coordinate, which limited them and made them better suited to nighttime.

Kui Xin thought about it for a long time and couldn't bring herself to fuse away either of them. She decided to shelve the decision and wait until a day came when she really had to upgrade one of them.

With her abilities more or less sorted and everything worth upgrading upgraded, Kui Xin turned to Amber. "Let's try it."

"Okay." A glowing blue light lit up in the center of Amber's pupils.

A faint red light began to glow in Kui Xin's eyes too, the telltale sign of a mental supernatural ability in use.

Amber's mind stretched out like a strand of spider silk and touched the barrier around Kui Xin's thoughts... She struggled to rein in Mental Immunity and lower her brain's firewall, letting Amber's mind connect to hers. It was like linking two computers with a data cable.

The two cables found the right ports, and with a click, Kui Xin's and Amber's minds were connected.

"It worked? Wait, it seems like..." Amber's surprise flooded instantly into Kui Xin's head.

His emotions were so strong and so real that, swept up in them, Kui Xin almost couldn't tell whether they were coming from Amber or were her own.

At the same time, Amber was picking up Kui Xin's emotions: confusion, bewilderment, a total loss of what to do... She was extremely clumsy at controlling mental abilities, and her brain was wide open right now. If he went any deeper, Amber could even have touched her memories.

Amber leaned back and cut the mental link at once. "You don't know how to control it yet..." he said quickly.

He chose his words carefully. "While we're linked, I can sense everything you think, and you can sense everything I think. We can talk heart to heart. But that's way too... way too..."

"Way too intimate," Kui Xin said, the corner of her mouth twitching. "And way too unguarded."

"Right!" Amber nodded hard, his expression serious. "It can be controlled. You just have to learn how. Until you do, we need to be careful about linking. Sometimes even the faintest passing thought in your head gets sent over to me..."

He seemed even more nervous and flustered than Kui Xin, who had just tried a mental link for the first time.

Amber had never formed a mental link with anyone but Obsidian. In that brief moment, he had felt as though he'd barged into someone's private space, while Kui Xin had barged right into his.

Put another way, it was like two people who had only just met moving at breakneck speed, so that in no time at all they were suddenly close enough to share a pair of pants and sleep in the same bed!

Amber was deeply uncomfortable with it.

"So that's linking done. What about synchronization?" Kui Xin asked. "I've never felt it. What does it feel like?"

"Synchronization is when two minds become a single whole. It's not like what we just did. Our minds were still two separate individuals. Your mind and mine can link, but they can't synchronize... which is more or less what I expected," Amber said.

"Oh well," Kui Xin said. "So I really can't count on this to get rid of Angel's mental coordinates. Still, maybe Mental Immunity could..."

"Mental Immunity?" Amber asked.

"An ability I picked up by chance," Kui Xin explained.

Mental Immunity might be able to block the mental coordinates for a while, like putting a running tracker into sleep mode so its location signal couldn't be sent out.

The problem was that while Kui Xin could block her own, she had no way of blocking the coordinates in Amber's mind along with them.

"Anyway, let's rest first. You're hurt worse, so you sleep and I'll keep watch. We'll take turns after that," Kui Xin said.

"Okay." Amber lay down on the bed.

Before long, his breathing had grown slow and soft.

Once Amber was asleep, Kui Xin took a vial of blood-red liquid from inside her clothes and held it up to examine it.

It was Augus's blood. But now wasn't a good time to read his memories, however badly Kui Xin wanted to know what secrets they held. She was in terrible shape and needed to recover for a while before she could safely read them.

She had come to Floating Port City for three main reasons: to rescue someone, to kill Depriver 777, and to investigate the Dark Realm.

One of the three was done now, which left two.

"Don't rush... don't rush..." Kui Xin told herself. "Keep a clear head. Acting on impulse will only get me killed."

To the sound of Amber's even breathing, Kui Xin calmly opened an encrypted connection on her communicator and began... studying.

She couldn't read the memories yet, but she could just about manage some studying. At the very least, she could memorize a few more molecular formulas.

Matter Reorganization was a truly murderous ability. She wondered whether Harriman's Matter Annihilation demanded this much knowledge from its user too.
