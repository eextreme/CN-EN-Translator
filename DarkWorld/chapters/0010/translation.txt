"Meeting over?" Amber asked when he saw Kui Xin step out of the spatial vortex.

"It's over. No hiccups." Kui Xin walked over to the unconscious Samanto. "Anything wrong with him?"

"I went over him carefully. No trackers, no locators," Amber said.

This was a different base. Kui Xin had set up plenty of backup bases, more than enough for holding prisoners.

Electronic rings had been fitted around Samanto's neck, wrists and ankles. His jaw hung crooked to one side, and blood dripped steadily onto the floor. Since he was a supernatural ability user of roughly B-rank, and no weakling, Amber had taken an extra precaution and dislocated every joint in his arms and legs.

Having been trained by Mechanical Dawn, Amber was a professional at this sort of thing.

"Did you find anything?" Kui Xin asked.

"I read his surface memories. This guy is no ordinary character," Amber said. "I originally assumed the Federation had turned him, that they'd promised Samanto something in exchange for going undercover in our organization. But after reading his memories, that doesn't seem to be the case. Samanto belonged to the Federation from the very start. He's been active in bounty-hunter circles for five or six years, and from the moment he got into that world, he was a Federation mole. He reports directly to the Special Intelligence Division."

"The Special Intelligence Division?" Even Kui Xin was a little surprised. "So his real identity is a Special Intelligence Division agent? An undercover agent?"

Amber nodded. "That's right. The Special Intelligence Division trains certain specialists to infiltrate particular circles and work undercover so they can catch whoever they want caught. Samanto was clearly one of the deeply buried ones. He just had bad luck and ran straight into you. His ability is actually B-rank. He probably claimed C-rank to keep a low profile, and got caught out."

"He's a rare talent, then," Kui Xin said. "Can you mentally control a B-rank Awakened?"

"I can, but it's a bit tough. It'll take five or six consecutive mental intrusions, and I'll have to redo the consciousness implant every so often to reinforce the control. The exact number depends on his willpower. It's a long process, close to a week," Amber said. "If Obsidian were here, three rounds of implanting would be enough, and we could cut the time down to three days."

"Then I'll leave it to you. Do your best," Kui Xin said.

Amber asked, "What concept should I implant? Loyalty to Black Snake, to Fer-de-lance, or to the Sea Without Light?"

"Loyalty to the Sea Without Light," Kui Xin said.

"Got it," Amber said.

If mental supernatural abilities were ranked, Amber's would undoubtedly fall into the higher tier: no medium required and broadly versatile. Kui Xin's Soul-Bewitching Voice could also achieve mental control, but hers stripped the controlled person of all initiative, turning them into a puppet that could only run on preset instructions. They acted only when she gave an order.

Amber's mental implanting was different. On some level it completely rewrote the controlled person's thoughts, so that they kept their own way of thinking while defecting wholesale to another side. Amber could turn people into puppets that thought for themselves.

Before Amber began the implant, Kui Xin took out a syringe, bent down and drew a tube of Samanto's blood.

"Oh, right, Amber," Kui Xin said. "Do you know where Obsidian is?"

Amber hesitated. "Just as Angel can only sense the rough direction of a coordinate, I can only sense his rough direction. I can only tell exactly where he is when we're close enough. At that distance, Obsidian and I can communicate directly by telepathy."

"Where do you think he's being held?" Kui Xin asked.

"If I were Eve, I'd lock him up in the safest, most secure place there is..." Amber said. "He's very likely in the Federal Administrative Center. That's the place Eve truly controls."

"Then getting him out will be hard," Kui Xin said. "I'd need an S-rank ability."

"Is there anything I can do?" Amber said softly. "Please, just say the word."

"I want to kill Night Cicada, and first I need to know where he is," Kui Xin said. "Use your mental ability to control enough small animals as lookouts and help me find him. Keep an eye on what Trailblaze Technologies is up to."

"All the glass at Trailblaze Technologies is one-way, and the ventilation ducts carry high-voltage current. Not even a rat could crawl through," Amber said.

Kui Xin mused, "The ventilation ducts are out... so what about the sewers?"

A question mark slowly took shape in Amber's mind.

"I suggest you have a rat go up the toilet drainpipes," Kui Xin proposed with complete rigor. "They can't possibly electrify the drainpipes, people still need to flush. So there can't be any barriers inside the pipes either. You steer the rat up along the pipe all the way, then have it pop out of a toilet bowl..."

The question marks in Amber's mind multiplied.

"With any luck, the rat won't get swept away by a sudden flush. We'll just have to take our chances," Kui Xin said.

Amber: "..."

He said with difficulty, "I understand... Maybe my thinking's just been too narrow. That route never once occurred to me... I'll do my best to try it."

"I'm counting on you, Amber." Kui Xin seemed to have full confidence in him.

Hearing "leave it to you" yet again, Amber couldn't quite say how he felt. Besides a small flicker of joy at being trusted, there was perhaps a tiny bit of some subtle, complicated emotion too.

What normal person would ever come up with something as bizarre as crawling through the sewers?!

...

In the lounge, Kui Xin took out two tubes of blood.

One was Samanto's. Amber had said Samanto belonged to the Special Intelligence Division, but Kui Xin still needed to read his memories to confirm the details.

Without further hesitation, she squeezed blood from the glass tube and let it drip into her mouth.

Blood Spirit activated, and Samanto's memories came rushing in...

As a young man, Samanto had been a tall, strapping lad. The face in his memories looked nothing like his current one. Back then he wasn't called Samanto, either. His name was Jamien. Samanto was actually an alias.

Jamien came from a fairly comfortable family, and his parents had paid for him to go to university. But while he was still studying, they died in a car accident, and he had to take out loans to finish his last two years.

After graduating, Jamien passed the assessment and joined the Special Intelligence Division. He had no supernatural abilities, so he worked a desk job. It wasn't a happy one. The veteran staff constantly pushed him out, and he even lost his shot at a promotion.

The Special Intelligence Division was a department with a complex structure. Internal competition was fierce, chances to climb were few, and the only people who really mattered were the Awakened.

The atmosphere at the Special Intelligence Division filled Jamien with resentment, but he couldn't afford to lose the job. He wanted to seize any chance to climb the ladder and make more money so he could pay off his loans.

Then a windfall dropped out of the sky and landed on Jamien's head: he Awakened! He thought his chance at promotion had finally arrived, but what came next hit him like a bolt from the blue. The Special Intelligence Division's leadership decided to send him out as an undercover agent.

His pay would be multiplied many times over. The catch was that from then on, he'd be living on a knife's edge.

After careful thought, Jamien took the job. If he turned it down, he'd be paying off the rest of his loans until he was seventy or eighty. If he accepted, he'd only need to work until his thirties before retiring with honor and a hefty pension.

He underwent plastic surgery and, under the Special Intelligence Division's arrangements, changed his name and took on a new identity. From then on he was no longer Jamien. He was a bounty hunter who knocked around the streets.

His main job was fishing. Using his dual identity as both a Federation fugitive and a bounty hunter, he would make contact with other wanted criminals on the run, then coordinate with the Special Intelligence Division to kill them when they least expected it. Once they were dead, he could openly cash them in for the bounty money.

That way, everyone else would just think "Samanto" was an excellent bounty hunter who'd completed lots of jobs and killed lots of people. It would never cross their minds that he might be an undercover agent.

Jamien spent several years living on the edge, one step from death, and his loans were nearly paid off.

And that was when he received a mission.

His superior told him, "Finish this one job and you can retire early. You're not even thirty yet. You'll have loads of leisurely days ahead of you, and the pension the department's giving you is a huge sum!"

Jamien believed him. He planned to finish this one last job.

What he never in a million years expected was that this last job would blow up in his face.

Kui Xin surfaced from the memories and rubbed her throbbing temples.

This undercover agent Jamien was a hapless guy, too. Still, his memories had some value. From them, Kui Xin worked out how parts of the Special Intelligence Division operated and also learned how Jamien made contact with the Division.

They used a special code similar to Morse code. Entering a specific string of digits on a communicator would connect to a covert line, and then entering the encrypted intelligence would send the message out.

The Special Intelligence Division wasn't within Adam's remit. Secrets like these, known only to insiders, were probably not something it knew much about.

Next up were the memories of the person who had died so unfortunately during the interview.

Because the deceased was suspected of being a player, Kui Xin took these memories very seriously.

She let a drop of blood fall into her mouth, and Blood Spirit activated.

Kui Xin's vision blurred, and chaotic images crammed themselves into her head. Unlike Jamien's memories, this interviewee's were a complete mess.

The images in her mind kept flickering, switching to the next before she could make out the last. In fact, while reading the blood, Kui Xin saw not just one person's memories but five or six people's in a row. The memories were twisted and stitched together like a jigsaw puzzle that had been scrambled and reassembled, chaotic and without order.

Within seconds, Kui Xin's head felt like it was splitting, and she was forced out of the memory reading.

Rubbing her head, she got up from the chair and stared darkly at the vial of blood in her hand.

Kui Xin hadn't been able to read anything useful. She couldn't even find out the interviewee's name, because the blood was contaminated. Worse, the interviewee hadn't been transfused with just one person's blood but with blood from at least five or six different people, which was why the memories it held were so chaotic.

It confirmed one of her suspicions: the organization behind that interviewee had to be Mechanical Dawn.

Only Mechanical Dawn would have guessed that Kui Xin had killed Red and taken Red's Blood Spirit. They knew the flaw in Blood Spirit, so they had pumped the interviewee full of blood to keep Kui Xin from getting any intel out of it.

Planting a highly dangerous mechanical device inside a human body was also very much Mechanical Dawn's style.

Kui Xin had no doubt that Mechanical Dawn had sent the interviewee, who was probably a player, and then blown his heart up just to disgust and provoke her. Kui Xin had provoked Eve before, so now Eve was paying her back in kind.

Once she'd worked that out, Kui Xin took several deep breaths, pushed down her anger and got her head back on straight.

She checked the time. It was already afternoon.

Amber's scouting with the rats would take time, and Kui Xin meant to use it to rest and recover her strength. She had opened spatial vortexes several times today, and she wanted to be back at full strength before she felt comfortable going after Night Cicada.

"Please, please don't leave too soon," Kui Xin murmured to herself as she lay on the bed, just before she closed her eyes. "If you're gone, where am I supposed to find you?"

"You mean Night Cicada?" Adam asked.

"Yes," Kui Xin answered.

"You make a death sentence sound like a love letter," Adam said. "I hope you get what you want."
