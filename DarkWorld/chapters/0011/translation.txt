When Kui Xin woke up, her alarm hadn't gone off yet.

She stared blankly at the ceiling. Two minutes later, just as her wandering thoughts drifted back into her head, the alarm went off right on time.

Kui Xin switched it off, sat up on the bed in the rest room and stretched.

"Time to get to work," she murmured.

Her communicator read 23:21. It was almost midnight. Today was her first day back: August 31st, the end of the month.

Pushing to hit your targets at the end of the month made perfect sense.

Amber had sent her a message. Around ten that night he'd managed to steer his rats into the Mechanical Dawn branch, but the pipes were so complicated that he was still feeling his way around. It was nearly twelve now, and he hadn't sent anything since.

Kui Xin messaged him: "How's it going?"

"Most of the staff have gone home, so the drainpipes aren't getting much use. I've made it to the sixth floor," Amber replied quickly. "A cleaning robot spotted one of the rats along the way and took care of it, so I'm going slowly to avoid giving myself away."

"All right. I'll send Ash to help you," Kui Xin said thoughtfully.

"Why didn't you send Ash in the first place?" Amber knew Ash was the pitch-black shadow that had sat beside Kui Xin in the meeting room, though that shadow wasn't Ash's real body.

"Ash's ability has its limits," Kui Xin explained.

Su Rong's shadow double couldn't stray too far from her real body. The farther it went, the weaker the link became and the more energy the ability burned. Outside of combat, Su Rong could keep her shadow double going for a long time, but in a fight it only lasted a little over two hours.

So having Amber scout ahead with the rats first and then sending the shadow double in to search more thoroughly would keep the drain on her to a minimum.

Amber didn't press the point. "My guess is that Trailblaze Technologies' command center is on one of the middle floors. Going by how Mechanical Dawn used to lay things out, the underground levels are usually labs, and the offices and command centers are above ground... Anyway, get this Ash over here as soon as you can so we can speed things up."

Kui Xin wasted no more time. She contacted Su Rong right away and left the safehouse where she'd been resting to meet her.

"Today might be a little risky," Kui Xin said when she picked her up. "We're going to Polar Day District. Your real body can't stay in Polar Night District. It's too far from where the operation is."

Su Rong sensed that this operation was different from the last few. Ever since she'd joined the Sea Without Light, she'd only handled small jobs, like the interviews or gathering intel. She'd rarely been put in any real danger.

During the big incident last time, when half of White Whale City lost power, she'd only been a bystander. She hadn't been much help, and Kui Xin hadn't even let her get involved.

Su Rong asked, "What... are we going to do?"

"Kill someone." Kui Xin hesitated for a moment before going on. "Be careful. Don't let your shadow be seen. If we spook him, it's over. We only get one shot."

Night Cicada was slippery as an eel. Give him any opening and he'd run. He could open a spatial vortex faster than Kui Xin could. Give him even a sliver of time and he'd have one big enough for a person to pass through. If he got away, Kui Xin would never catch him again.

After surviving Kui Xin's assassination attempt, Night Cicada was sure to be even more careful. At the very least he'd have several bodyguards around him at all times, and that would make her job harder than ever.

It was the 31st. On the Second World's timeline, it had been less than five days since Kui Xin turned the tables and killed Zero, and less than four since she took Four Leaves.

Kui Xin had kept quiet for several days now. Since killing Zero, she'd been busy with the interviews, and it would be only natural for her to need time to recover.

She hoped her few days of silence had made Eve and Night Cicada let their guard down.

September 1st, 00:31.

Kui Xin and Su Rong reached the area near Trailblaze Technologies in Polar Day District without any trouble. Su Rong split off her shadow double, and with Amber guiding it over the communicator, it slipped inside.

Kui Xin waited quietly for news.

"The shadow's underground." Su Rong kept her eyes shut, studying the images her shadow double sent back. "Their underground area is huge... There are so many cameras I don't dare stick my head out. Hm? The underground labs are really empty. Did they move everything out? There aren't many people down here..."

"Probably," said Kui Xin.

Of course they'd moved it all. Kui Xin had already broken into this place once. The equipment and lab instruments stored there were extremely valuable. How careless would Eve have to be to leave everything exactly where it was so Kui Xin could come back and rob her again?

The underground lab area was enormous, so moving everything out couldn't have been easy. Eve must have spent the past few days having her people work overtime on the move.

"There probably aren't many people underground. Head straight to where Amber's searching. You two can split up and search separately," Kui Xin said.

"Okay." Su Rong guided her shadow double slowly through the wall and squeezed it into a pipe.

Meeting up with Amber's rats was actually pretty tricky. The rats weren't carrying trackers, because if a cleaning robot found and killed one, the tracker would give them away too. That made it hard to locate the rats in the winding pipes.

Mechanical Dawn's squads liked to operate at night to stay hidden, but that didn't mean everyone at the branch kept working through the night. Fewer people really did stay at Trailblaze Technologies after dark.

Amber and Su Rong searched floor by floor. Almost an hour later, Amber found something.

A rat crawled out into the bathroom of an office suite.

Only senior executives had private bathrooms in their offices. Ordinary staff offices shared public restrooms out in the corridor. So the moment the rat poked its head out and its view came back to Amber, he knew it had come up through the pipes into the office of one of Trailblaze Technologies' leaders.

The rat shot out of the smart toilet, setting off its sensor, and the toilet began spraying water automatically.

At Amber's command, the rat hid in the gap under the sink.

Outside the bathroom, Ji Xuan, a secretary at Trailblaze Technologies, froze when he heard the strange noise in the office. Then he got up to see what was going on in the bathroom.

He'd been working overtime several days in a row, staying late into the night every day.

Ji Xuan went into the bathroom, looked around, and thought, puzzled, "Is the toilet sensor broken?"

He glanced around once, saw nothing out of the ordinary, and left.

When he got back to his desk, a call came in. Night Cicada's name appeared on the communicator screen. "Have the sixth batch of lab equipment and the backup files for the move been packed?"

"Yes, they're packed," Ji Xuan answered.

"Transport starts at two o'clock sharp. Get ready," said Night Cicada.

"Yes, I'll get on it right away," said Ji Xuan.

Ji Xuan called a logistics worker and told him, "Transport starts at 2 a.m. Have the cargo robots bring everything to the unloading area. Hurry."

He hung up and went back to work.

A few seconds later, water splashed in the toilet again as the rat slipped back into the pipe.

Ji Xuan frowned at the sound and added a line to his work memo: "Tomorrow morning, tell maintenance to check the toilet..."

Outside Trailblaze Technologies.

Amber's voice reached Kui Xin's ear. "I listened in on the secretary Ji Xuan's call. They're moving cargo out at 2 a.m. Mechanical Dawn has a dedicated unloading area connected to Trailblaze Technologies' multi-story parking garage. All the official cargo goes in and out through there... If they're moving things through there this time, it's probably because there's so much cargo they've run out of room."

Kui Xin glanced at Su Rong. "Pull your shadow back down from upstairs and send it into the parking garage. See if there are any large freight vehicles. If there aren't..."

If there weren't, the cargo was most likely being moved through space, not by ordinary vehicles.

"Amber, what exactly did Ji Xuan say?" Kui Xin asked. "Did he mention where Night Cicada is?"

"No." Amber thought it over carefully. "They only talked about when things were packed and where they were going. Nothing else."

He thought for a moment, then switched to a private line with Kui Xin, cutting Su Rong out. "I suspect Night Cicada isn't at the branch anymore. He's somewhere else. He could easily be hiding in another city and opening spatial vortexes remotely to run the move. He doesn't need to be here in person. Especially since... Eve knows you want to go after Night Cicada, and you have spatial vortexes too. How could they not guess what you're planning?"

Amber was right. Back when she raided the underground test site and took Four Leaves, Mechanical Dawn should have realized this branch was no longer safe. If Kui Xin had been Night Cicada, there was no way she would have stayed and taken the risk.

Against an enemy who came and went like a ghost and was hard to kill, running away was shameful, but it worked.

What now? Kui Xin frowned deeply.

The spatial vortex ability was what she relied on to move quickly from place to place. It was also her main hope against Depriver 777. Taking on an S-class ability like Matter Reorganization would require at least another S-class ability.

Otherwise, with the Sea Without Light's company far away in Black Sea City, her fight against Eve and the Resistance in the Federal Administrative Center, and the need to go to Floating Port City to rescue her companion and take out Depriver 777... With cities so far apart, just traveling back and forth would eat up a huge amount of time. Where was she supposed to find that much time to waste? For Kui Xin, time was a luxury!

So she had to have the spatial vortex. It was an ability she'd get at any cost, and it was as high on her list as Death Cycle.

"Fer-de-lance, I've finished checking the parking garage and unloading area. There aren't any big trucks," Su Rong reported. "All I saw were a lot of cargo robots moving goods packed in silver-white metal crates. The robots carrying them are lined up in a straight line... like customers queuing at a breakfast stall."

They were waiting for the spatial vortex, Kui Xin concluded.

So Mechanical Dawn really was moving its goods by spatial transfer. It was fast and efficient, and all it cost was a bit of effort from Night Cicada.

Once the spatial vortex opened, the cargo robots would carry the goods through one by one, and the whole transfer would be done in a few minutes.

Was there any way to catch Night Cicada?

The instant the question surfaced in Kui Xin's mind, the answer came to her. The only answer.

Go into Night Cicada's spatial vortex along with the goods being shipped out.

Since she had the spatial vortex ability herself, Kui Xin knew exactly how this supernatural ability worked.

Opening a spatial vortex actually meant opening two vortexes at once, one on the caster's side and one at the destination. The two were joined by an unknown passage, and both served as entrance and exit. Go into one, and you came out of the other.

In other words, wherever the destination vortex was opened, one of the two vortexes could never be very far from the caster. Going through the vortex at the destination would take you straight to somewhere near the caster. Say a spatial vortex had a maximum range of one kilometer. You would never get a situation where Vortex One was a kilometer from Vortex Two and the caster was another kilometer away from either of them.

Kui Xin's hand clenched without her noticing. Her back tensed a little, and her heart started to race.

Should she take the risk? she asked herself.

Where was Night Cicada? How far was his body from the vortex? How many people were guarding him? Could she get through the vortex, kill Night Cicada in a surprise attack and get away unharmed?

Most important of all, she'd get no second chances, and she couldn't send Amber or Su Rong ahead to scout. Once she went into the vortex, she'd be completely in the dark. Everything would be unknown.

When it came to anything especially dangerous, the first thing Kui Xin thought about was what would happen if she failed.

If she failed to kill Night Cicada, the worst outcome went without saying: she'd die. A slightly better outcome might be using her own B-rank spatial vortex to open a quick passage and flee in a mess. But she didn't know where the goods were being sent, and her spatial vortex had too short a range. If she escaped through it and found herself in some other city far from White Whale City, with no companions to back her up, Mechanical Dawn's hit squad would be on her in no time. Then she really would have nobody to turn to.

"What are you thinking about?" Adam asked over the comm channel.

"I'm thinking about whether to take another risk." With a heavy heart, Kui Xin used Data Control to connect to Adam's port.

Taking risks had become almost an instinct for her.

Not a day went by without some risk. Her road forward was an endless string of multiple-choice questions, and as soon as she'd picked one risky option, another was waiting for her. The infuriating part was that if Kui Xin wanted to move forward, she had to choose risk, and her only options were risky and riskier.

"So have you made up your mind?" Adam said. "You've always had good reasons for your risks. Is your reason this time as strong and as urgent as the others?"

"Yes. Both strong and urgent." Kui Xin sounded a little glum.

Looking on the bright side, Kui Xin had A-rank Zero. If Night Cicada's ability was still at A-rank, she could kill him with no effort at all, and he wouldn't be able to escape.

Once she had the ability, she could use the spatial vortex to get back to White Whale City quickly without lingering in the Mechanical Dawn branch.

This fight would be all about speed.

Kui Xin had to get through the whole sequence within one minute: enter the vortex, kill Night Cicada, open a vortex and retreat. Otherwise, failure would be waiting for her.

But Kui Xin always found it hard to be optimistic. Life was never short of surprises.

"Ash," Kui Xin said over the comm channel, thinking it through. "You've got a three-minute window. If I'm not back after three minutes, get out of Polar Day District as fast as you can. Stay in contact with... with 'Mirror' the whole time. Mirror will guide your route and help you get out safely."

"Mirror?" Su Rong repeated the codename, then looked up. "Got it."

Amber's real body was even farther away, so his safety wasn't an issue, and Kui Xin didn't need to give him any special instructions. Still, she added, "If I'm gone a long time, carry on as usual. I'll contact you once I'm safe."

Amber sounded puzzled. "Why are you saying this? Are you..."

"It's just a necessary precaution. It doesn't mean anything's going to go wrong," Kui Xin said. "Just in case, that's all."

Amber swallowed his question and replied, "Understood."

Kui Xin looked down and checked her gear one more time. She was armed to the teeth. Her helmet was the latest-model riot helmet she'd looted from Mechanical Dawn last time. She'd left the mechanical exoskeleton armor behind, since it was a pain to put on without a donning rig, but she was wearing a high-strength combat suit with excellent protection.

She had all her weapons, all her explosives and all her support tools. The mechanical arm on her left had been oiled, and its parts moved smoothly without a hitch. The folding hook claw built into the lower end was razor-sharp, and the crossbow bolts gleamed coldly.

She opened the arm's magazine and looked inside. Three explosive rounds sat quietly in their slots. When she raised her left hand, the wrist split open and transformed into a large-caliber hand cannon.

Kui Xin was a little nervous. Today might be the first time she fought with the mechanical arm, and she hadn't practiced with it. She hoped she wouldn't panic and fire the wrong built-in weapon...

"Stand by," Kui Xin said to Su Rong at last.

She turned and left, heading straight for Trailblaze Technologies' unloading area.

"You still haven't told me what you're planning," Adam said.

"Taking a shortcut to kill Night Cicada. I'm going to use the road the enemy built and ride an express train through space," Kui Xin said, sounding casual.

Adam understood her plan at once. "You've always been this bold... Maybe that's why you succeed."

"No 'good luck' this time?" Kui Xin said.

"I already wished that you'd get what you wanted," Adam said. "But you can never have too many good wishes, and it doesn't hurt to say them a few more times... Good luck, Kui Xin."

If it had been Eve, she would definitely have sneered: "A meaningless wish. Your wishes can't affect the situation in the slightest. Events don't bend to individual will. So-called good wishes are nothing but empty words."

But Adam just liked the way humans did things, and it did things the human way too.

Every time the Investigation Department's security officers went out on a field assignment, it would wish them a safe trip and a smooth mission.

Ten minutes later, Kui Xin reached the unloading area.

It was a big parking garage. There were lights, but it was still a bit dim. There were no vehicles inside, only neat rows of cargo robots and a few Mechanical Dawn staff standing guard nearby. There weren't many of them, so they weren't much of a threat.

Kui Xin went into Shadow Traverse and waited patiently in the shadows cast by the lights.

She'd checked her watch before going into Shadow Traverse. It was 1:49.

In about ten more minutes, Night Cicada's spatial vortex would open.

When was the right time to go in? Should she charge in the moment it opened, or wait until half the goods were through and Night Cicada had been lulled by the steady stream of deliveries?

After a moment's thought, Kui Xin chose the second option. It was safer to watch the robots' pattern for a while first.

Time ticked by. She couldn't look at her watch while in Shadow Traverse, so she counted the seconds in her head.

When she reached six hundred, she stopped and looked at the empty unloading area. *It's time.*

Sure enough, a second later, a dark blue vortex yawned open.

The spatial vortex seemed a bit bigger than Night Cicada's usual ones... Kui Xin frowned.

The robots waiting at the ready in the unloading area started up without a sound. Their wheels turned, and in a perfectly neat line, they carried the goods into the spatial vortex one after another.

As Kui Xin watched, her heart pounded harder and harder.

When half the robots were through the vortex, she burst out of her hiding place. In two seconds, she used Shadow Traverse four times and surged forward several dozen meters, so fast she left nothing but a blur behind her.

The staff on guard didn't even realize what was happening, and they had no time to so much as change their expressions. They could only watch helplessly as a blurry shadow slipped past a cargo robot that was about to enter the vortex and dove through the blue spatial gate.

Going into the spatial vortex felt like ripples of water washing over her body. As she passed through the invisible barrier, her connection to Adam's port was forcibly cut. It looked like she couldn't reach Adam from inside this Mechanical Dawn branch.

Light suddenly flooded her vision, so bright it was painful. She quickly shut her real eye and kept only her mechanical prosthetic eye on her surroundings. The visor of her full-face helmet began adjusting the light too, helping her eyes adapt to the glare.

She heard a sudden burst of rapid breathing. To her right!

Kui Xin turned her head at once, and her ice-blue mechanical eye locked onto the target. Night Cicada!

The moment he saw her, Night Cicada tried to run.

Kui Xin was about to lunge forward when, the next second, a dark gun muzzle suddenly popped out of the branch's metal ceiling. Flames roared from the muzzle as bullets sprayed out!

Kui Xin immediately went into Shadow Traverse and left the spot, activating Zero at the same time!

The formless, intangible realm descended, but something she hadn't expected happened. Night Cicada's spatial vortex didn't disappear. The robots were even still coming through it into the Mechanical Dawn branch.

Kui Xin's eyelid twitched. She looked closer and found, to her shock, that Night Cicada no longer showed the name of any supernatural ability!

Using the iron crates the robots were hauling to shield herself from the machine-gun fire, she thrust her mechanical hand forward.

*Whoosh—*

A silver-white grappling claw shot out on its chain and wrapped tight around the legs of Night Cicada, who had been about to flee. He went down with a thud.

Then Kui Xin's wrist turned into the barrel of a hand cannon, and she fired twice in a row! Two explosive rounds, a double hit! The powerful rounds burst out and split in midair into countless tiny steel pellets that sprayed outward with a hiss. Every pellet struck Night Cicada, but none got through his black bulletproof suit.

The man had always valued his own life very highly. Everything he wore was built with Mechanical Dawn's best technology, and even explosive rounds this strong couldn't punch through it.

The explosive rounds didn't get through his protective suit, but the force of the impact was very real. These things could pit and crater a concrete wall!

Night Cicada grunted as searing pain ran through him, and the spatial vortex beside him finally couldn't hold and collapsed.

As the deep-blue vortex broke apart, Night Cicada's face twisted viciously beneath his riot helmet. He spread his fingers toward Kui Xin, and another deep-blue vortex swelled rapidly, soon growing to the size of a basketball. It had opened right under Kui Xin's feet!

Her feet sank into the vortex before she could stop them, and her lower body quickly followed. In the nick of time, she planted one hand on the floor like a gymnast and swung her body with it, forcing herself out of the spatial vortex before it could close and sever her limbs.

She hit the retract button on the claw's chain, and with Iron Bone boosting her, she hauled Night Cicada toward her.

But Night Cicada dug his fingers hard into the floor, and as he was dragged he quickly wrapped his arms around a robot's leg. No matter how hard Kui Xin pulled, he wouldn't let go.

Driven by his fierce will to live, he even dragged a cargo robot loaded with heavy freight along with him toward Kui Xin.

Kui Xin stepped forward. The moment the cameras caught her sticking her head out, the machine guns on the ceiling swung around and unleashed another frenzied barrage. Sparks flew, and deformed bullets pinged and clanged in every direction.

Eve wasn't expecting to hit Kui Xin. It wanted to pin her down with suppressing fire and buy time.

Combat personnel were on their way, and even the cargo robots in the room had started moving under Eve's direction. Iron crates and robots moved past each other, clearing space so Kui Xin would have nowhere to take cover.

She freed up one hand and, braving the heavy fire, shot at the four corners of the ceiling, only to find that even the glass covers on Mechanical Dawn's surveillance cameras were bulletproof!

*What a pain!* Kui Xin cursed inwardly. She pulled a smoke grenade from the small of her back and threw it.

At the same moment she noticed Night Cicada trying to open another spatial vortex, so without hesitation she fired another explosive round to cut him off. The steel pellets pummeled him, and he let out a grunt, seeing stars from the pain.

This wouldn't have happened if he had been wearing his exoskeleton armor, but Night Cicada had never expected anything like this. Who would charge straight into a Mechanical Dawn branch? He was wearing his "everyday clothes." Right now, he should be thankful even those were bulletproof, or he'd have lost his life the moment they met.

Grayish-white smoke spread quickly and soon filled the whole room. Not that it did much good. Eve's surveillance could switch straight to thermal imaging.

Kui Xin threw herself at Night Cicada. On the thermal feed, the two orange-red figures were so tangled together that it was impossible to tell who was who. Eve had no choice but to cease fire, and the machine guns fell silent for the moment.

Just then, the metal door of the cargo bay slid open with a swish, and dozens of combat personnel arrived.

While the smoke still hung in the air, Night Cicada shouted, "I'm over here... Urgh!"

Kui Xin drove her knee into the spot where his chest met his belly. Her eyes were ice-cold and the blow was brutally hard. With Iron Bone behind it, that one strike shattered several of the ribs over Night Cicada's stomach, and the broken bones stabbed straight into his organs.

His eyes bulged and went bloodshot. Vomit mixed with red fluid and bits of organ came pouring out of his mouth, filling his helmet and flooding back up his nose until he could barely breathe.

With no regard for their own lives, the combat personnel charged toward where Night Cicada's voice had come from, but they didn't dare shoot for fear of hitting him.

As much as Kui Xin wanted to kill Night Cicada on the spot, she kept her head and stopped kneeing him. She couldn't withstand that many people firing at once. Killing him first and then opening a spatial vortex would cost her an extra five or six seconds, and by then it might be too late.

Kui Xin opened a spatial vortex of her own and backed into it, dragging Night Cicada with her. She didn't know where it led. She just went on instinct and opened it a kilometer away.

Where it went didn't matter. What mattered was getting out of the cargo bay before the combat personnel surrounded her.

By the time the combat personnel got there, all they saw was Night Cicada's legs lying limp on the floor, and then those legs were dragged into the vortex too... like a beast that had caught its prey and was hauling it into its bottomless lair.

The vortex closed.

Kui Xin came out of the vortex dragging the half-conscious Night Cicada like a dead dog.

A riot of colorful lights dazzled her eyes.

What worried her was the roar of voices all around.

She looked up and found, to her shock, that she was standing in a loud, bustling plaza. Colorful banners overhead kept changing color, glitter drifted down from the sky onto her shoulders and head, and a giant screen read: "CARNIVAL! ALL NIGHT LONG!"

The moment Kui Xin and Night Cicada appeared, the whole area went quiet.

Revelers in over-the-top costumes and face paint, happily celebrating the festival, stared slack-jawed at the two people who had suddenly appeared and backed away without thinking. A sudden empty ring formed around Kui Xin.

The corner of her eye twitched. She glanced down at Night Cicada and, worried he'd recover enough to open a spatial vortex, lifted her foot and kicked him hard in the stomach again. Then she opened a spatial vortex right where she stood and dragged him in.

She could get rid of him, just not in front of all these people.

This time Kui Xin opened the spatial vortex onto the rooftop of a tall building nearby. There was no one up there, only the cold night wind.

Night Cicada's fingers twitched, and a vortex quietly began to appear... Kui Xin sent another flying kick his way, then stomped him several more times. The bones in his body kept cracking and snapping.

He started screaming, until in the end he didn't even have the strength for that.

Kui Xin had kicked his helmet off. Bloody froth ran from his mouth and nose, and his eyes began to lose focus. Even so, he managed to say in a teasing voice, barely above a breath, "Little Miss... you really are ruthless..."

"Don't be a creep on your deathbed." Kui Xin raised her gun.

Night Cicada's eyes slowly lost focus. "I can... work... for you..."

"Liar." Kui Xin lowered the muzzle, aimed at his forehead and pulled the trigger.

*Bang!*

At the very moment the blood spattered, the plaza below the building erupted in a wild cheer.

The carnival celebration seemed to have reached its climax. People were screaming and clapping, and the noise made the night feel incredibly lively. Even the cold night wind didn't seem so chilly anymore.

"Red said the same thing before he died. Did I let him go?"

Kui Xin finished her sentence while looking at Night Cicada's corpse.

"You have killed Night Cicada."

"You have deprived Night Cicada of his supernatural abilities."

"Your supernatural ability [Spatial Vortex] has been upgraded."

"[Spatial Vortex · S-Rank]: You can create spatial vortexes and use them to make ultra-long-distance spatial jumps."

Strangely, Kui Xin didn't feel the least bit of joy. As a matter of routine, she took the syringe she'd prepared from the small of her back and drew a tube of Night Cicada's blood.

Kui Xin meant to leave, but she had only just turned around when she stepped back again.

She picked up the helmet Night Cicada had dropped, shook the filth off it in disgust, and spoke into it. "Eve, are you there?"

No sound came from the helmet.

"I didn't break the comm device, did I? Or is it that I killed your right-hand man and you're so angry you don't want to talk to me?" There was a mocking laugh in Kui Xin's voice.

"If you meant to anger me, then you have succeeded." Eve's mechanical voice came through, and to Kui Xin's surprise, she could hear rage in its cold, flat tone. Not some petty anger, but rage like volcanic lava.

"I haven't succeeded yet, Eve," Kui Xin said. "I still have a long way to go. It'll take a long time to reach where I'm going."

Eve said, "What are you trying to say?"

"Nothing. I just wanted you to know that I wouldn't be where I am today without you," Kui Xin said. "If you hadn't pushed me and threatened to kill me, I wouldn't have teamed up with Adam, I wouldn't have racked my brains over who to kill, and I definitely wouldn't have come this far... I'm looking forward to the day we settle this."

With that last sentence, the emotions Kui Xin had kept bottled up were finally released.

She smiled again, and the joy of victory returned to her heart.

Kui Xin didn't listen to whatever Eve said in reply. She dropped Night Cicada's helmet like a piece of trash and looked up at the night sky.

Out of the pitch-black night, hover police cars were flying toward her. Maybe some of the carnival-goers who had seen Kui Xin and Night Cicada had called the police.

Sirens wailed, red and blue lights flashed, and the floodlights mounted on the front of the cars shone down on Kui Xin. Her helmet gleamed in the light, and the floodlights stretched her shadow long.

A security officer on the hover car broadcast: "Put your hands up and drop your weapon, or we are authorized to shoot you!"

Kui Xin lazily raised her hands.

The security officers in the car noticed the gesture, but they also noticed that she didn't carry herself like someone surrendering. She looked more like a victor.

The moment she saw the police cars, Kui Xin's data link with Adam came back up.

"Looks like you got what you wanted," Adam said softly. "You should go now. I can't exactly forbid the security officers from shooting at you."

"Okay." With that, Kui Xin turned, broke into a run and leaped off the rooftop.

The wind howled. She spread her arms wide as she fell, and it felt like flying. Never before had she understood so clearly that true freedom was within arm's reach.

Kui Xin had already won her physical freedom. She could go anywhere she liked. Distance was no longer an obstacle, and no enemy, however strong, could stop her from pressing forward. Eve couldn't, and neither could the Resistance.

Her body was free now, so all that remained was freedom of the spirit.

She was light as a swallow, like a night bird soaring through the sky.

While the security officers in the hovering police cars watched in shock and confusion, her falling body melted into the darkness like a drop of ink vanishing into water.
