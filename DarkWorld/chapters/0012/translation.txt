And that was how the feud between Night Cicada and You Wang began.

"Don't you want to ask me which character my surname actually is?" You Wang said, just to needle him.

"No need. I read the name tag on your nightstand." Night Cicada held grudges, and for days on end he barely said a word to You Wang.

But he kept a close eye on Ghost King, and not only because of their feud. It was also because of what Ghost King had said.

Why shouldn't he ask, or dig any deeper? Where had the missing children gone? Hadn't they been adopted?

Ghost King seemed to know something, but no matter how Night Cicada fished for hints, he always dodged the subject, and he was very good at changing it.

Night Cicada was still young, after all, and as time went on he put the matter out of his mind. Life at the orphanage kept him busy enough. Every day there were lessons and homework, and Mother Director made them do exercises too, saying they needed to build strong, healthy bodies.

Life went on like that for a while, and then the next checkup day arrived. This time the physical came with a psychological evaluation. As usual, the orphans lined up in the corridor outside the infirmary, and after that they were to report to the counseling room to fill out forms.

They went in one by one and came out one by one.

When You Wang came out of his physical and psychological evaluation, something was clearly wrong with his face.

Before Night Cicada could work out what that look meant, it was his turn to go into the infirmary.

Once the full set of tests was done, the man in the white coat who had examined Night Cicada looked over his results and tapped the electronic record board in his hand.

On his way out, Night Cicada looked back and craned his neck, trying to see whether he'd gotten a check mark or a cross this time. His results had always been check marks before. This time… it was a cross!

Night Cicada's heart lurched, and the hairs on his back very nearly stood on end.

His limited experience and his gut told him the same thing: this was not good!

Exactly why it was bad, he couldn't have said.

When all the children had been assessed, the white coat brought the electronic record book to Mother Director. She slid her index finger across the screen, then turned her head and said something to him and nodded. The white coat smiled and nodded back.

Night Cicada watched the scene from a distance, and goosebumps rose all over his skin for no reason he could name.

"Looks like we're in trouble," You Wang said behind him.

"What happens to the ones who get a cross?" Night Cicada asked quietly.

"I don't know, but it's definitely nothing good," You Wang said.

Night Cicada said, "How do you know it's nothing good?"

"Because I can do things other people can't," You Wang said under his breath. "Those kids weren't adopted… They went somewhere really bad."

"We're both in trouble, so why are you still keeping secrets?" Night Cicada folded his arms and looked at him. "Come on, tell me. Maybe we can figure something out."

"Fine… I can latch onto other people like a ghost… It's my secret. I've never told anyone." You Wang sighed. "I wanted to know where those kids went, so I possessed one of them… But he wasn't picked up by adoptive parents. He got into a black transport truck… like the prison trucks in cartoons…"

"What an amazing power," Night Cicada said, briefly getting sidetracked before quickly getting back to the point. "So he got into the truck, and then what?"

"The kid I was possessing passed out," You Wang said. "When I woke up I was back at the orphanage… Then I had a high fever for three days, and my nose wouldn't stop bleeding."

Night Cicada remembered that. "Oh, right… you were stuck in the infirmary for days. Why did you get a fever?"

You Wang said uncertainly, "Maybe… an aftereffect? The price of using a superpower?"

Night Cicada shivered. "Can we… run away?"

"Run away to where? Do you have money? Do you have your own account? What would we even do once we got out?" You Wang said. "Without an electronic account we can't even ride the rail tram, and we can't buy food."

Night Cicada's shoulders slumped in defeat.

Mother Director didn't give Night Cicada and You Wang the chance to plan an escape. That same afternoon she announced to all the children that Night Cicada and You Wang were being adopted and wouldn't be coming back to the orphanage.

That evening the orphanage served an extra-special dinner, as was the custom. Whenever a child was "adopted," there were two extra meat dishes that night.

Night Cicada pushed a few mouthfuls of rice around, tasting nothing.

Later that night, Mother Director called Night Cicada and You Wang aside and smiled at them. "Someone's here to pick you up. But before you go to your new home, you'll need a more thorough checkup. Our equipment here isn't good enough, so you'll have to go to a proper hospital…"

She seized both of them firmly by the wrists and walked so fast she practically dragged them out of the orphanage. On the road in front of the building sat a black transport truck that blended into the night. Two people in white hazmat suits stood guard beside it.

You Wang stopped in his tracks as fear spread through him. He started struggling with all his might without even thinking, and Mother Director nearly lost her grip on him.

But the people in hazmat suits came over carrying a pitch-black baton. The moment it touched You Wang's body, he shuddered and toppled over stiff as a board.

Night Cicada froze in terror. He backed away in a panic but couldn't break free of Mother Director's grip. "Stay away from me!" he screamed.

A small deep-blue vortex flashed into the air and vanished. The two people in hazmat suits exchanged a startled look, and one of them, without a moment's hesitation, jabbed the stun baton into Night Cicada. He went down stiff as a board too.

Before he blacked out, he heard muffled voices: "What's with these two kids? They seem to know something. They wouldn't have reacted like that otherwise."

"He's awakened. Awakening at this age is far too early. It'll do serious damage to their physical development. They're very prone to premature aging…"

"Either way, he's not a reject anymore… Report it up the chain and see what the higher-ups want to do."

When Night Cicada woke up again, everything in front of him was a blur of white.

He sprang up off the floor and found himself locked in a silver-white room. The four walls fit together without a single seam. There wasn't even a door.

He felt his way around the walls in a panic, circling the room several times, and shouted, "Is anyone there?"

With a click, a gap suddenly split open in the seamless wall. Night Cicada jumped back in fright and landed on his backside.

Before him stood a white-haired old woman in a white coat and gold-rimmed glasses. Old people usually seem kind and gentle, but this woman's brown eyes were cold and hard.

She was clearly in the twilight of her life, yet her gaze was sharper than a young person's, and more ambitious.

Her badge bore a name: Chen Zhiqiu.

"Wh-who are you?" Night Cicada stammered.

"Your researcher," the old woman said. "From now on you'll call me Dr. Chen. You'll be living here permanently, cooperating with my experiments and my work."

It all felt absurd to Night Cicada, and he still couldn't quite make sense of it. "Why? Why do I have to be here? Where's You Wang? What… experiments?"

The old woman smiled, but the smile did nothing to soften the lines of her face.

"While you're here, there are a great many rules you'll need to follow. The first rule is obedience. Don't ask questions," Dr. Chen said. "Now, come with me. I need to run a preliminary test of your ability."

Night Cicada had already learned obedience at the orphanage. He sensed, dimly, that disobeying would only get him hurt, so he swallowed his fear and confusion and followed Dr. Chen out of the silver-white room.

They walked down a long corridor. It was silver-white too.

As they passed one stretch of the corridor, Night Cicada suddenly heard horrible screaming. It was faint, as if it came from very far away… He stopped short, fear spreading across his face.

The screams came one after another. It didn't sound like one person screaming, but many people screaming together, men and women, old and young, their voices tangled into one. The sound seemed to pierce straight through the mind and strike at the deepest fear in his heart, and it made his skin crawl.

"Am I going to die?" Night Cicada had gone deathly pale.

He was only ten, and for the first time the heavy word "death" surfaced in his mind.

"No." Dr. Chen's gold-rimmed glasses caught the light as she smiled. "Not if you're obedient enough. Not if you're valuable enough, either. You've already proven obedient enough. No crying, no screaming, no making a fuss. Very good… Now let's confirm just how valuable you are."

Fortunately, and unfortunately, Night Cicada turned out to be valuable enough.

His supernatural ability was of the extremely rare spatial type, and Dr. Chen looked at him as though he were a priceless treasure.

"What we can say for certain now is that you're valuable enough," Dr. Chen said meaningfully. "Until you lose that value, you'll stay alive, child."

"Where's You Wang?" Night Cicada couldn't stop himself from asking.

Dr. Chen said, "Who's You Wang?"

At her question, Night Cicada's scalp prickled. After the ability test he'd just been through, he had a vague idea of what supernatural abilities were. Only people with supernatural abilities counted as valuable, and only they earned the right to go on living. But Dr. Chen didn't know that You Wang had supernatural abilities too! Without any value, You Wang would die!

"He has an ability too, he told me!" Night Cicada stared hard at Dr. Chen. "So he gets to live too! If you have an ability you get to live, right?"

Dr. Chen raised an eyebrow. "Oh?"

She took the comm handset off the wall, dialed a number, and said to the person on the other end, "That boy named You Wang, has he been disposed of? No? Good. Bring him here."

When the call was over, Dr. Chen looked down at Night Cicada with an expression that was hard to read. "There. Just as you wished, he gets to live."

Night Cicada let out a breath of relief.

Unfortunately, he had celebrated too soon.

In his memories, the years he spent at the experimental base could only be described as a life without daylight.

Every day he was injected with a whole pile of unidentified drugs. He had a cannula in his arm. With the cannula, they didn't have to keep poking new holes in him.

Because his ability had awakened too early and he underwent ability training so often, his stamina could never keep up. His cell activity was also far lower than a normal Awakened's. His ability and his body were developing in opposite directions. Under all the drugs and training his ability grew by leaps and bounds, while his body grew weaker and weaker, until a few steps left him out of breath.

But because he was valuable research material, Dr. Chen gave him the best drugs available and even developed several new compounds to boost his cell activity. Whenever a drug was still in development, Dr. Chen always tested it on plenty of other subjects first. Only once she had confirmed it was safe, with relatively mild side effects, would she use it on him.

All of it was meant to keep him alive longer.

The experiments were agony. Some drugs had few side effects but caused tremendous pain. Some brought on fevers that wouldn't break. Some made the whole body ache violently and the muscles spasm. Some caused hallucinations...

Electric shocks were one way to stimulate cell activity, so every few days Night Cicada also had to undergo electroshock therapy.

It wasn't as if Night Cicada had never thought of escaping with his spatial vortex. But there were at least five or six safeguards on his body.

There was a mechanical device installed in his spine, another in his brain, and another on his heart. His wrists and ankles were locked in specially made high-tech shackles and cuffs, and he wore an electronic collar around his neck.

The moment he did anything out of line, he'd be dead on the spot.

Night Cicada never saw You Wang, and Dr. Chen wouldn't tell him anything about him.

Sometimes Night Cicada felt regret.

"If I hadn't told Dr. Chen about You Wang's ability back then, maybe he could have gotten out of this."

In his third year at the experimental base, Dr. Chen mentioned to Night Cicada, as if in passing, "That subject called You Wang has been transferred."

"Why was he transferred?" Night Cicada asked.

"He's dying," said Dr. Chen. "You two awakened at about the same age and your physical conditions are similar. Every drug we used on you was tested on him first. He hasn't been able to hold out for a long time."

Night Cicada stayed silent.

This base terrified him, and he hated it. A fire of vengeance burned inside him, but he didn't dare let even a flicker of it show.

The researchers here were utterly cold. They treated the test subjects like pigs in a pen.

All Night Cicada could do was quietly endure.

Two more years passed like this. Just when Night Cicada had given up hope and gone numb inside, a turning point arrived out of nowhere.

Dr. Chen was getting on in years by then. She was quite old, yet she still threw herself into her research every day. Someone her age should have retired long ago to enjoy her twilight years, but she hadn't.

On a perfectly ordinary experiment day, Night Cicada lay on the lab bed, his face blank, watching the drip slowly fall from the IV bag.

Dr. Chen stood beside him, watching the instruments and recording data. She calmly said, "Do you want to leave this cage?"

The question came so abruptly, and her tone was so ordinary, that for a moment Night Cicada didn't register what she had asked.

Not until she asked again: "Do you want to leave the cage?"

Then Night Cicada reacted. The corner of his mouth curled stiffly, and he said in a mocking, offhand tone, "If I say yes, will you let me go?"

Dr. Chen didn't have the power to let him go. She was only a researcher, not the one actually in control of the base.

"What if I told you I could?" Dr. Chen smiled.

Night Cicada's heart pounded so hard it nearly leapt out of his chest.

"Why so kind all of a sudden?" He knew there was no such thing as a free lunch. "What do you want me to do? What are you getting out of this?"

"Someone has paid on your behalf. I've already gotten what I wanted, so there's nothing more you need to give me." Dr. Chen looked down at him. "All I need from you is one answer."

"You know what I'll choose," Night Cicada said.

"If you say no, I'll put a lethal dose into your IV line. You'll die, and I'm confident enough that nobody will ever trace it back to me," said Dr. Chen. "You don't get to choose between freedom and no freedom. You only get to choose between death and freedom."

Night Cicada's jaw tightened. Then he suddenly laughed out loud. "At least you've given me the freedom to choose death. Is that supposed to be 'mercy'?"

Dr. Chen looked at him without a flicker in her eyes. "Choose."

Night Cicada answered almost without thinking. "I choose freedom." He laughed again. "Does freedom forced on you by someone else even count as freedom? And what price do I have to pay for this so-called freedom?"

"You'll pay a price, but it will be far lighter than staying in this base," said Dr. Chen.

Dr. Chen added an anesthetic to Night Cicada's IV line, and he sank into a deep sleep.

When he woke up again, he saw an unfamiliar ceiling. It wasn't the cold, silvery white ceiling of the lab. It was an ordinary grid ceiling, the kind he'd seen back at the orphanage.

Several wounds on his head and body throbbed dully. The mechanical devices in his brain and body seemed to have been removed.

"You're awake." A voice echoed through the room. It was a man's voice.

Night Cicada managed to turn his head and saw a man in his thirties sitting on the sofa, watching him.

"I'm Kui Haidong, leader of Mechanical Dawn," said the man. "I'm the one who had you rescued."

"Dr. Chen works for you?" Night Cicada was surprisingly calm. "You got me out. What do you want me to do?"

"Work for us, and in return you'll have a great deal of freedom." Kui Haidong's gaze was deep, and he spoke bluntly. "The base that ran those inhumane experiments on you belongs to the Federation, and the Federation is exactly who we're fighting... You can get your revenge on them. What we need is your ability."

"Is that so?" Night Cicada said quietly. "Dr. Chen is a cold, rational researcher. She said someone paid on my behalf, and that's why she got me out... What did you pay her? What kind of payment could move someone like her?"

Kui Haidong said gravely, "Immortality."

He paused, then added, "The human body is made of cells. It has limits, and mortals can never reach immortality. What we gave her was immortality on the level of data."
