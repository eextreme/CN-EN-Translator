"Augus." Kui Xin called him by name.

At the same moment, she unleashed Zero. An invisible field descended, suppressing some of Augus's abilities.

Augus noticed, but he didn't seem to care in the least. He even had the leisure to smile and say, "The sun has gone out."

"It's wonderful to be able to talk to you without any obstacles. The language barrier was such a nuisance." He spread his arms in a welcoming gesture. "You came all this way just to find me. It must be so."

Kui Xin got straight to the point. She didn't want to waste a single second.

"How did you get out of the Dark Realm?" she asked. "Who's backing you?"

She really had no desire to keep playing along with Augus's games. He was a hundred percent certain to have guessed that she'd come for these answers, so she could skip the probing stage. Asking outright might get her more information.

"You're too close to see it clearly, Ma..." Augus remembered that wasn't Kui Xin's real name and corrected himself on the spot. "Deprivator No. 233."

"Too close to see it clearly?" An idea came to Kui Xin.

Did Augus mean that the way out of the Dark Realm was simple, and she just hadn't thought of it?

The answer came to her almost at once: midnight, and multiples of seven.

Zero and seven were both special numbers. At midnight, monsters would come out of the Dark Realm and charge into the outside world, and at the hours that were multiples of seven, Menneto might come out to wander. If Kui Xin wanted to get out of the Dark Realm, she would probably need to time it to a special window too.

Or perhaps leaving the Dark Realm required meeting some special conditions as well... such as making that choice between the ruby and the sapphire.

Otherwise, Augus would have had no reason to go rushing off to Tongling City.

Kui Xin had already half suspected as much last time. Augus's words had only confirmed it further.

Now she still had to pin down a few details. First, was making the Scales choice a necessary condition? Second, could you enter and leave the Dark Realm at midnight every night, or did the entry and exit times also run on a fixed seven-day cycle?

The timing probably wasn't that strict. The first mass outpouring of variant species from the Dark Realm hadn't happened on a Return Day. Besides, after Augus made the Scales choice and crossed over to the Second World, he likely wouldn't have had time to burst out of the Dark Realm right at that moment. He might have stayed inside for another day and only come out at midnight on September 1... That made sense too.

He had been out of the Dark Realm for only about twenty-four hours. The containment dome at the Floating Port City Nuclear Power Plant had been breached only yesterday, and discovered only yesterday, which was why there was only a construction crew there and no Federation troops. In so short a time, the crew couldn't have finished surveying the site. They still didn't know what was inside the containment dome.

It wouldn't be long before the Federation reacted. The crew had gathered beside the containment dome. Living people couldn't go deep inside the radioactive dome, but machines could. If they sent robots in and the robots happened to come into contact with the Dark Realm... the moment the Federation got word, it would probably surround the place.

The Dark Realm, the meeting point between the First World and the Second World... and Kui Xin's body was still inside it.

"I'm curious. Does the Federation not know that a Dark Realm has appeared in the Second World?" Kui Xin asked.

"They know something, but they think it's just the machinery inside the plant malfunctioning," Augus said offhandedly. "Some of the gauges failed, the radiation meters gave abnormal readings, and the bigwigs assumed there would be a nuclear explosion. It never occurred to them that a Dark Realm would show up here. A nuclear power plant makes a fine umbrella. All they can see is the plant. They have no idea what terrible thing has appeared under its containment dome. The Federation locking down the news here was the right move, if only by accident... But the secret won't stay hidden for long. The Federation is about to find out about the Dark Realm. Maybe they already have, and they're working overtime to get people in place."

Kui Xin's face darkened.

"You're nervous." There was a sickening malice in Augus's voice. "You must have worked it out by now. The necessary condition for making the Scales choice is entering the Dark Realm, and the necessary condition for leaving the Dark Realm is making the Scales choice. If the Federation finds out there's a Dark Realm hidden in there, how will you get inside to make your choice? How will you break through the Federation's dragnet?"

He burst out laughing. "Sorry for causing you a little trouble."

So the necessary condition for leaving really was making the Scales choice!

Kui Xin's expression turned exceptionally grim.

But then it occurred to her that the Scales choice might not be a necessary condition at all. Augus might be deceiving her. He wanted to see her lose control...

"Is that so?" Kui Xin said. "Whether I can leave or not, I'll verify for myself when I go back."

Augus laughed again. "As they'd say back in your home country, you're the type who won't turn back until you've run headfirst into the wall, Deprivator No. 233. Feel free to think I'm lying to you. But even supposing you can leave the First World's Dark Realm, so what?"

"The First World has more than one Dark Realm," Kui Xin said in a low voice. "The Dark Realms in the Second World correspond to them, so there must be more than one here too."

"You know roughly where the First World's Dark Realms are, but what about the Second World's?" Augus spread his hands. "In this world you don't know well, in this world under the Federation's tight control, where will you get your intel? And even if you get it, how will you get inside?"

He had done it on purpose.

Kui Xin's heart sank.

She hadn't understood before why Augus had insisted on breaking the leak-proof containment dome when he left the Dark Realm. Now she did. He had wanted to draw the Federation's attention and lure them into the dome to discover the Dark Realm.

Once the Federation surrounded the place, getting in would be incredibly hard for Kui Xin. And once the Federation discovered the remaining Dark Realms, they would certainly seize control of those as well.

Augus was putting obstacles in Kui Xin's way. He was smugly showing off what he'd done, hoping to see her face erupt in fury. That would give him a thrill.

"I can put off making that choice for now." Kui Xin stayed calm.

"But you don't get to choose," he said, savoring it. "If you don't go in, your body in the other world will be trapped there forever. Alone, in dead silence, with variant species coming out to roam. In the Dark Realm you lose your supernatural abilities, so you'll be nothing but food for them."

Kui Xin's brows knitted tightly.

There wasn't a single crack in what Augus said, and Kui Xin had no idea whether he was telling the truth. Judging by their previous encounters, Augus didn't lie most of the time; at most he made himself sound mysterious... But that "doesn't lie" label might just be a disguise, a shell he used to lull his enemies.

Kui Xin said nothing. Not getting to see her angry face, Augus was a little disappointed.

"I have to say, you ruined my plans, Deprivator No. 233," he said strangely. "I was originally going to choose the sapphire... But my body in that world was too badly hurt, and you were standing guard right beside it..."

Inside, Augus was nowhere near as calm as he looked. He was still sore about having his plans disrupted, and deliberately leaking the Dark Realm to the Federation was his revenge.

But why? Kui Xin had always assumed that, for someone like Augus, the Second World was more interesting, and he'd rather stay there.

"You don't like the Second World?" Kui Xin eyed him strangely.

"No, I like it very much." Augus gave a smile that didn't reach his eyes. "But liking it comes with conditions."

Liking it comes with conditions... Kui Xin thought of herself. The Second World was rotten to the core, but it did have a kind of twisted charm: technology, supernatural abilities, and a few fairly decent people.

The condition for Kui Xin liking this world was that her life and freedom not be under anyone's threat. Only then could she accept this world.

Was it the same for Augus?

Had someone threatened his life and taken away his freedom, so he wanted to give up the ruby and choose the sapphire?

"Tonight still isn't lively enough." Augus took a step back. "There's a lot of truth you want to know, and I happen to be carrying the truth you're after. If you want it, then come play another game with me... Try to catch me in this city. How about it?"

He tapped the ground lightly with his foot, and the hard gray road actually began to heave like ocean waves! The gray asphalt had turned unbelievably soft, like a bog in a swamp. Anyone standing on it would sink right in!

Startled, Kui Xin left the ground in an instant with Shadow Walk and landed on her toes atop a streetlight pole by the road. But the streetlight immediately began tipping over to one side as the road beneath it softened, and at the same time sharp stone pillars shot up from underground like the bared fangs of a wild beast, their razor tips aimed straight at Kui Xin's vital points.

Matter Reorganization could even change the physical form and properties of matter!

Kui Xin's body turned to mist, dodging the stone spikes, and she flashed more than ten meters away with a string of Shadow Walks.

She looked up and saw Augus give her a leisurely wave. He even took off his mask and mouthed "Bye" at her.

Augus's body slipped into the shadowed side of a building. The walls of the buildings around him swiftly dissolved into flying ash. He moved among the buildings as if strolling in his own back garden, and any matter that came near him crumbled away completely.

The area within ten meters of Augus became a void, with only the ash of disintegrated matter swirling through the air. The streetlights went out, their paint peeled away, and then the poles themselves began to melt away... And Augus's figure was moving farther off.

He had truly lost his mind! This was the city center, and the Investigation Department's people hadn't left yet! Using an S-class ability so recklessly here was bound to put the Federation on alert, and Kui Xin would be exposed to the Federation's eyes along with him.

Augus was about to get away. Kui Xin spread her fingers, and a deep-blue spatial vortex opened beside her left hand while another opened right behind Augus's head. The instant the spatial channel formed, her mechanical left arm clicked, and explosive rounds blasted out of the muzzle in her arm!

The shots were deafening. Kui Xin was terrified they wouldn't be powerful enough to kill Augus where he stood. If he got out of Zero's range, his A-class Flesh Regeneration would heal his wounds instantly.

Ignoring the heat building in the barrel of her hand cannon, she fired three shots into the spatial vortex in the space of a single second!

There was a thunderous boom.

The steel pellets burst outward like fireworks into the spatial vortex beside her, then sprayed out of the channel on Augus's side in a dense cloud that engulfed him.

And then something jaw-dropping happened: the pellets stopped dead in midair!

Augus snapped his fingers, and the pellets from the explosive rounds vanished like a stain washed away by water, crumbling into flying ash along with everything else disintegrating around him.

It dawned on Kui Xin that in their skirmish back in the First World, Augus hadn't come close to showing the true power of his S-class Matter Reorganization.

Every S-class ability had its own specialty. Kui Xin's spatial vortex could carry her absurd distances, while Augus's supernatural ability was so overbearing it simply defied reason!

"Exposing ourselves in the middle of Floating Port City does neither of us any good," Kui Xin said, raising her voice.

Augus grinned, and the grin was especially hateful. "No, it doesn't do us any good... I just wanted to drag you down with me."

Drag her down with him? Kui Xin had no time to think about what he meant, because Augus suddenly turned and broke into a run. The grey matter that had melted into the air swiftly gathered beneath his feet into a staircase that bore him upward, carrying him all the way up to the hover tram track above the city.

He stood on the track and looked down at Kui Xin, his voice full of twisted arrogance. "Aren't you going to come after me, Deprivator No. 233? Or don't you have the guts?"

Standing that high up, he looked as though he had every pedestrian below under his heel. Augus gazed down on her with eyes full of provocation.

The wail of the hover patrol cars' sirens was right in their ears, and their lights swept across the ground toward Augus, but he didn't budge, as though the Investigation Department didn't even register with him.

He meant to raise hell in Floating Port City, consequences be damned!

As if he was afraid Kui Xin wouldn't give chase, he added pointedly, "If you can't catch me tonight, I'll leave Floating Port City and go wherever I please. I'll hunt down the players one by one and kill every last one of them. If you back down now, you'd better be ready for what happens next... This is your only chance."

Augus wanted to make Kui Xin angry.

And Kui Xin... Augus had succeeded.

When was the last time she had been this furious? Her face was frozen solid, but inside her a fierce rage was blazing.

When Ball Python tried to assassinate her? On the Kraken? When Eve threatened her? Or when she was planning to fake her death and Eve and the Resistance killed her over and over again?

"Who do you think you are, Sun Wukong wreaking havoc in Heaven?" The veins at Kui Xin's temples throbbed wildly, and her urge to kill swelled to its peak.

At that moment, the killing intent she felt toward Augus far surpassed anything she had once felt toward Zero.

She flung out her left arm. The grappling claw, trailing a silver steel cable, struck the hover track. The claw clamped tight, the cable reeled in, and her body shot upward with a whoosh.

Using the momentum, Kui Xin grabbed the edge of the track and vaulted up with one hand. The iron-plated soles of her tactical boots rang sharply against the rail.

She straightened up on the track.

Now she and Augus were eye to eye.

Augus clapped. "Not bad. That's more like it."

For a moment, the air and time itself seemed to freeze.

Two great beasts had met on the wilds. After a round of feeling each other out, they circled one another, watching for an opening, searching for a weakness. Neither dared to strike rashly; the slightest rustle was enough to set their nerves on edge.

"Please, Adam, cut off the city's surveillance," Kui Xin said silently through Data Control.

"Already on it."

Adam's answer eased her mind for an instant, but then an even greater anxiety swamped her.

Because she had no idea why Augus was doing this. Why did he want to make such a huge scene? Drag her down with him... what did that mean?

Kui Xin cleared away the distracting thoughts and reined in her churning emotions.

Augus had said that if she didn't catch him tonight, he would leave Floating Port City. Kui Xin couldn't afford to gamble on whether that was true. Augus had to die. Whether or not he left Floating Port City, he had to die tonight!

A hover patrol car rounded a building, its white-hot searchlight about to sweep across Augus. Gun barrels tilted up, and in the very second before the bullets locked onto them, Augus took a step back and, of all things, broke into a sprint along the hover rail.

He was unbelievably fast, faster than the world's greatest athletes! He was like a cheetah racing along the rail... no, he was even more agile than a cheetah.

But Kui Xin was no slower. Her muscles stretched out as she unleashed her top speed, keeping her balance as she raced along the edge of the rail after Augus's fleeing figure.

She was running so fast that the wind howled in her ears and whipped her fringe off her forehead.

Her tactical boots scraped against the steel track with a shrill screech that rattled her eardrums. Whenever she braked to change speed, a string of dazzling sparks burst from beneath her feet.

They rounded a bend and left the Investigation Department's patrol cars behind. Hover patrol cars simply weren't built to fly and aim at a target at the same time in the crowded skies above the city.

The hover tracks crisscrossed the sky above the city, with more than one line stretching across it.

The vast shadows of the skyscrapers fell over the tracks.

Augus was just too fast! Worse still, he kept channeling Matter Reorganization as he ran. Wherever he passed, the hover rail didn't disintegrate entirely, but it became rotten through. Several times Kui Xin stepped on corroded steel plates and nearly fell, and the gap between them gradually widened.

Kui Xin did a quick mental calculation. At their current distance, if she used Shadow Walk to close in, she would be forced back into physical form inside the zone controlled by Matter Reorganization while still some way from Augus himself. She would need one more jump.

That one extra jump could very well leave her badly injured.

She had to get close to him some other way, then use Shadow Walk to reach his actual body and take him down in a single blow.

Kui Xin narrowed her eyes. With the help of her electronic prosthetic eye, she precisely mapped the rail's course and the straight-line distance. Still running, she opened a spatial vortex twenty meters ahead of her, and another in the air above the path Augus would have to take.

Overhead was a blind spot. When people ran, they mostly watched what was ahead and to either side, and almost never looked up.

The world's fastest sprinters covered ten meters a second. The moment Kui Xin's spatial vortex unfolded twenty meters ahead, she was already there.

She leapt in without hesitation. Passing through the thin barrier, she dropped out of the vortex on the other end, just as Augus was passing right beneath her!

Kui Xin immediately slipped into Shadow Walk and sliced into the domain of Matter Reorganization like a keen steel blade. Then she took physical form right behind Augus, driving Iron Bone to its absolute limit!

Bullets and blades would be disintegrated, so she would fight him with sheer physical strength.

Every bone in her body creaked under the strain, and her muscles bulged. But at that very moment, her high-strength bulletproof jacket and her exposed skin instantly began to dissolve; even the strands of her hair were coming apart! Blood-red muscle was laid bare to the air, only to be repaired again and again by Flesh Regeneration, healing and disintegration locked in a tug-of-war.

Bang!

Kui Xin's kick landed squarely on Augus, sweeping him clean off the hover track!

But the ash-like matter actually wove itself into a rope and stopped his fall.

Matter Reorganization wasn't only about disintegration. Naturally, it could also reassemble the broken-down matter into whatever form he wanted.

Kui Xin's eye twitched. She reached to the small of her back, unhooked the whole row of demolition charges from her belt, hurled them at Augus, and escaped with Shadow Walk.

Boom!

The flash of the explosion nearly flooded her vision. The entire string of charges went off at once, and the orange-red flames very nearly swallowed Kui Xin.

She raised her hand to look and found the surface of her metal left arm pitted and blotchy. A moment later and Augus's ability would have corroded it beyond use.

The same went for the weapons hanging outside without any casing to protect them; their surfaces were all pockmarked. That was exactly why Kui Xin had decisively thrown away every last demolition charge: she was afraid Matter Reorganization would set them off early and blow her sky-high.

Augus wasn't dead.

He was held aloft by the grey matter, with a dull grey substance like a metal shield in front of him. It wrapped around most of him like an eggshell and had blocked the shockwave.

Even so, Augus was badly hurt. Ordinarily wounds like these would have been nothing to him, thanks to Flesh Regeneration, but unfortunately that ability had been erased by Kui Xin's Zero.

And it wasn't only the wounds from the explosion. He also bore Kui Xin's wounds: thanks to Damage Sync, however much flesh Matter Reorganization had stripped from Kui Xin, the same amount had been stripped from Augus.

Her wounds healed rapidly, but Augus was practically drenched in blood. It dripped steadily down, staining the rail red.

"Huh..." Augus said, as if talking to himself. "What are explosives made of again?"

A strange look immediately came into Kui Xin's eyes.

So that was it... so that was it! It suddenly made sense to her. Matter Reorganization had conditions! To break something down and reorganize it, he had to understand its molecular makeup!

Her expression was quite something to behold.

If she remembered correctly, Augus was a dropout who'd never been any good at school.

In other words, Augus was nowhere near tapping the full power of this S-class ability. That was his weakness!

She couldn't get close to Augus, or her body would be broken down. She couldn't attack from range either, because anything metal would be broken down too... Wait. Having her body broken down wasn't necessarily a bad thing. She could combine Damage Sync with Flesh Regeneration and make Augus bleed to death.

But the timing would be hard to get right. Zero only lasted about half an hour.

Augus raised a hand, and the hover rail under Kui Xin's feet twisted like a dough twist. She lost her footing and almost fell out of the sky. A moment later a spatial vortex opened beneath her. She leapt into it and came out on another rail a little farther from Augus.

"About time... she showed up," Augus murmured.

"Kui Xin, a gunship is approaching." Adam was speaking faster now. "It doesn't belong to the Investigation Department. Judging by its paint scheme, it's Special Intelligence Division. I wasn't given any handover from the Special Intelligence Division beforehand, so I can't confirm who's on board."

"What?" Kui Xin frowned and looked up at the sky.

Augus looked up at the sky too.

Against the multicolored night, a black helicopter was flying toward them.

The helicopter was fast, and its cabin door slid open in midflight. A blonde woman in a silver-white combat suit stood at the edge of the cabin, her gaze locked on Augus, her eyes blazing with hatred and murderous intent.

"Who is she?" Kui Xin had a bad feeling about this. She granted access to her body camera. "Can you scan her face through my camera?"

"Yes." The texture of Adam's mechanical voice changed, and even Kui Xin could sense its surprise. "Deputy Director of the Special Intelligence Division. S-class Matter Annihilation. Your enemy was actually trying to lure her here!"

"This is an image reconstructed from the data. He looks pretty much like this in real life," Kui Xin said. "I'm leaving it to you. Find him as fast as you can."
