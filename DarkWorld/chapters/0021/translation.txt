"How many of them are there? How big is the cordon?" Kui Xin's voice was barely a whisper, little more than a faint breath, but Adam caught every word.

Data Control had stopped working too, so she had no way of talking to Adam through her mind.

"Right now there are thirty people gathered here. Ten are Special Intelligence Division agents, and the rest are Investigation Department security officers. More are on their way," Adam said. "The cordon is centered on your building and has a diameter of two hundred meters. As more people arrive, it keeps expanding, and so does the search area... They're also sending more drones this way."

A chill spread through her. Kui Xin felt as though she had sunk into a bottomless lake, the water pressure crushing her lungs until she could barely breathe.

"The data the drone swarm sends back passes through my filters, so you don't need to worry too much about the machines," Adam said. "What you need to watch out for are the people."

"That guy with the S-class Zero must be the Special Intelligence Division's trump card, right?" Kui Xin said. "How much do you know about him?"

"Very little. The man who just arrived is named Zhong Xinou. He's the Director of the Special Intelligence Division and an S-class Awakened. The official files don't say what his ability is. It's probably one of the Federation's top secrets—very few people know about it, and even fewer talk about it," Adam said. "When the Special Intelligence Division carries out missions in the field, it has to coordinate with the local Investigation Department. According to my database, Zhong Xinou has never been on a field mission and has never worked with the Investigation Department. He's a mystery, like a doomsday weapon the Federation keeps locked away... My guess is it's not that he's never been on field missions. He just doesn't bother telling the Investigation Department when he goes on one."

"This is bad," Kui Xin said. "We have no idea how long his ability can last..."

She took off her helmet, drew a dagger from the strap on her thigh, made a cut across her face, and then put the helmet back on. The last time, when she had killed Mechanical Dawn's artificial human Zero, she had used the same trick to tell whether her abilities had come back.

Kui Xin said, "Earlier, when I was fighting Augus, I had you block the surveillance. You don't need to anymore. They already know I exist."

"I'll cut out the key footage where your features are too recognizable and tell my superiors the surveillance equipment was destroyed. That's normal wear and tear in a fight this intense, so no one will be suspicious," Adam said.

"Good." Kui Xin switched channels to reach Amber. "How's the withdrawal going?"

Amber was panting slightly, his breathing uneven. He seemed to be running.

"Smoothly. No one's tried to stop me yet, and I'm far enough from the incident site," he said.

"Stay safe. Getting out comes first," Kui Xin said.

"Yes, understood," Amber replied.

Down below Kui Xin's building, the helicopter had already landed on the street, and Zhong Xinou, in full combat gear, jumped out of the cabin door.

"Kelly," he said evenly. "I don't want to lecture you about your recklessness again, but I'd like you to understand just how serious the consequences of tonight's actions are."

"I'll personally pay for the economic damage to the city center and the compensation for the victims," Harriman said. "As for public opinion, we'll say the city center was hit by a terrorist attack from a hostile force, and that I, as a Special Intelligence Division agent, arrived on the scene in time to eliminate the terrorist."

"Fine," Zhong Xinou said curtly. "Since you haven't caused any damage that can't be undone, the Special Intelligence Division will back you up on the PR side."

In just a few words, they settled how to deal with the aftermath.

Harriman said, "That man is dead, but only part of the body is left. Take it back for sampling and analysis. Also, the third person who was here... it's strange." Her gaze swept across skyscraper after skyscraper, searching for the enemy hiding in the shadows. "The Depriver didn't escape only because that person and I 'worked together.' She kept getting in his way, which gave me the chance to catch up to him."

"The third person's ability could counter that Depriver?" Zhong Xinou's brow twitched slightly. "According to our intel, that Depriver had a lot of abilities for saving his own skin, including a rare one that combined shadow and space. Once he used it, running away or staying alive would have been easy."

"That's what's strange. The Depriver never used that ability, not once from start to finish... It was like something was holding it back." Harriman turned her eyes to Zhong Xinou. "Just like you're holding back my ability right now. Tch. No matter how many times I go through this, I'll still say it—I hate your ability."

"But it's very useful," Zhong Xinou said. "Walk me through the whole fight from start to finish."

Harriman let out a breath, as if trying to expel the frustration and anger pent up inside her.

According to Special Intelligence Division intel, the male Depriver had been in and around Floating Port City for at least three weeks. During that time, he had hunted people with supernatural abilities and had attacked Awakened from the Investigation Department and the Special Intelligence Division several times.

Two weeks earlier, the male Depriver had killed Harriman's younger brother, who had been doing his PhD at Floating Port City University. When Harriman got the news, she was consumed with rage. Without a moment's hesitation, she applied for the mission and came to Floating Port City to hunt the Depriver down.

Over those two weeks, Harriman had come within a hair of catching Augus, but he was as slippery as an eel. The moment they came face to face, he slipped away using Shadow Realm. It was in that very clash that Harriman confirmed Augus's ability had most likely advanced to S-class. To make sure she could kill him, Harriman had asked the Federation to add Zhong Xinou to the hunt, but Zhong Xinou had too much on his plate to stay in Floating Port City the whole time.

Harriman said, "When I flew in, the Depriver was fighting someone else. Judging by her silhouette, his enemy was a woman, and her ability had to do with space. She could use a blue vortex to move around. The moment she saw me, she hid. During the chase, she stayed in the shadows the whole time, helping me cut him off. In the end, I wasn't the one who killed the Depriver—she was. Right before he died, the Depriver asked if I wanted information on another Depriver. And while we were facing off, he also mentioned that there was another S-class on the scene."

"Things are getting complicated," Zhong Xinou mused. "So what you're saying is, the first fight tonight was actually a showdown between two Deprivers. Their showdown drew you in, and you killed one of them. The other Depriver might still be nearby."

"She can teleport, so she might not be here anymore. But you activated your supernatural ability just in time," Harriman said. "Let's try a sweep. With you here, her ability should be suppressed too."

Zhong Xinou glanced at the time. "We have plenty of time. We can do a thorough sweep."

The drones were birds of prey hunting in flocks, circling the buildings and leaving no corner unchecked.

Zhong Xinou stayed where he was, but he gave orders to everyone on the scene to go into each building and search them more thoroughly.

The security officers in black combat suits moved in groups, filing one after another through the building entrances.

With her brother's killer dead, Harriman was much calmer. She went back over the fight in her head and sank into thought.

"According to the information we got from interrogations, the two Deprivers at the top of the pyramid are No. 233 and No. 777. We know the players widely assume No. 777 is male, which matches the Depriver who died tonight, so for now we can assume the dead one is Depriver 777," Harriman said. "In that case, the one fighting him was most likely No. 233. Not just any nobody gets to go up against a Depriver with an S-class ability. On top of that, right when I was about to kill No. 777, she beat me to it and killed him first. That was deliberate—she wanted to take his ability. So she's the infamous Deprivator No. 233. That's the only answer."

"A reasonable deduction," Zhong Xinou said, his face expressionless. "But there's one thing I don't understand. How was she able to suppress No. 777's other abilities? Does she have an ability similar to my Void Domain? And if she does... where did she get it?"

Harriman asked hesitantly, "Supernatural abilities can be inherited. Has anyone among your blood relatives awakened an ability similar to yours and been killed recently?"

"Not that I know of," Zhong Xinou said. "I don't have a big family like you do. I can count my relatives and friends on my fingers, and none of them fit what you're describing."

"Then maybe... it's a coincidence," Harriman said with a frown.

"Maybe," Zhong Xinou said, frowning as well.

Could there really be a coincidence like that? he wondered.

Zhong Xinou knew perfectly well how rare this ability was. After rising to his high position, he had looked through the Federation's Awakened registration records. In the past twenty years of the database, he was the only person who had awakened a Nullification ability.

How had Deprivator No. 233 found a second Nullification user and managed to kill them? The odds were even worse than finding a needle in a haystack...

"We have to get rid of Deprivator No. 233. She's bound to become a terrifying enemy... No, she already is one," Zhong Xinou said. "Two S-class abilities, plus a nullification similar to my Void Domain... As long as she wants to hide, we won't find her, and most Awakened are no match for her—unless I join the hunt personally."

"Let's hope she's still here," Harriman said. "Otherwise, we've got a problem."

Inside the building, Kui Xin's mind was racing as she searched for a way out.

Adam's mechanical voice struck at her heart. "The security officers have reached the second floor. You have to move up. I can filter the drones' scan data, but not the officers'. The scanners they carry project the scan feed straight into their helmets in real time, so there's nothing for me to filter, unless I shut their equipment down."

"Are any hover police cars landing on the roof?" Kui Xin asked, keeping her voice low.

"No. One small mercy: the way this building's roof is built, it can't be used as a landing pad," Adam said.

Kui Xin asked again, "Are there still helicopters or hover police cars in the air?"

"Five police cars. The helicopters have all landed," Adam said.

Kui Xin fished out her optical camouflage button. Luckily, the casing had only corroded a little. It still worked well enough, and once she put it on, it would still make her invisible.

She pinned the optical camouflage button to her clothes and went invisible.

The invisibility had its flaws. If she stepped into the light, her shadow would still show on the ground, so the searchlights from the hover police cars could give her away.

"Send a 3D map of this area to my eyepiece," Kui Xin said.

"Sent," Adam said.

Kui Xin studied the map for a while, memorizing the uneven heights of the buildings and where every levitation track ran. Adam had also marked the Investigation Department's police cars on the 3D map as little red dots, and the dots were flying along the buildings.

"Amber," Kui Xin called him, "are you out of range of Zhong Xinou's ability?"

"I'm out," Amber said. "I'm more than five hundred meters from him in a straight line."

"Good... I need you to take control of some night birds and send them into the police cars," Kui Xin said quietly. "Mental control that's already in effect can't be erased by Zero. As long as your body stays outside the domain, you can still make the birds fly wherever you want inside it."

"Amber, I need you to throw the hover police cars off their flight paths. I'll use the chance to slip past the blockade along the rails above the city."

Amber said, "All right. I'm on it."

Kui Xin let out a breath. Muffling her footsteps, she slipped past the security officers combing the floors and slowly worked her way up to the roof.

Her escape had to be stealthy, and it had to be fast. Otherwise she'd end up surrounded by two S-classes and dozens of well-equipped security officers.

Whether she lived or died came down to this one move!
