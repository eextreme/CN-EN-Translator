"We've done what we came to do," Kui Xin said. "Let's go, Amber."

"All right." Amber followed her into the spatial vortex.

But Kui Xin didn't use the spatial vortex to leave the city center. Instead she jumped to the roof of another skyscraper. The two towers stood at almost the same height with more than a hundred meters between them, and from where they stood they could see the roof of the Katerina Grand Hotel.

Kui Xin took an optical camouflage button out of her equipment bag and pinned it on. Her body instantly blended into its surroundings. Amber did the same, and he too vanished into the night.

The two of them stood side by side at the edge of the roof, gazing at the Katerina Grand Hotel in the night wind.

The words in the sky didn't stay lit for long. Five minutes later, every light in the hotel went out.

Someone had cut the skyscraper's power.

"They're out," Amber said. He didn't look the least bit surprised.

Floating Port City sat right next to the Federal Administrative Center and was under the Federation's tight control, so the words were always going to be wiped away the moment they appeared. He even suspected that hardly any media outlet would dare to report the leak at the Floating Port City Nuclear Power Plant tomorrow.

Any company that did would probably be facing ruin. Bankruptcy would be the least of it. In worse cases, life in prison or even losing their lives wasn't out of the question.

"One goes out, but there are other lights," Kui Xin said.

The next second, the laser lights atop another skyscraper not far away projected the words "The nuclear plant is leaking" again.

The laser lights on this skyscraper didn't last five minutes either before they went dark.

But right after that, the laser lights on one skyscraper after another shot into the sky, each spelling out the news of the nuclear leak in turn. The beams crisscrossed above the city, drawing everyone's eyes.

The lights came on one by one and went out one by one, like candles being blown out, only to flare up again, lighting up the dark city.

Every time a light came on, the building's power was cut completely. The darkness spread like a plague, infecting the steel giants that towered over the city center. Their bodies turned gray and dim, and the city lost its usual brilliance along with them.

"The people at the Federal Power Department must be run off their feet," Amber said flatly. "They must have been jolted out of a deep sleep by emergency alerts and started working overtime without even getting dressed. Whichever building's laser lights put up the message, they order that building's power cut."

"How lovely. On this not-so-pleasant night, we've got company staying up with us," Kui Xin said. "Watching people I can't stand have a bad time really improves my mood."

Adam said, "Stay hidden. Keep behind cover and don't let the scanners pick you up. Investigation Department patrol cars will be combing the area for suspicious people all night, and there'll be checkpoints on the ground scanning passersby."

Kui Xin's ears caught the sound of engines. She looked toward the noise and saw a whole line of hover patrol cars approaching through the sky. Each one was fitted with a powerful searchlight, black gun barrels jutted out from the sides, and red and blue lights flashed wildly.

Kui Xin looked away and said to Amber, "Let's go downstairs and lie low for a bit. There's too little cover up here."

The hover patrol cars were too close. Opening a spatial vortex here would give them away. They went into the elevator lobby on the roof, blocked the cameras, and only then used the spatial vortex to leave.

Following Adam's directions, Kui Xin opened the spatial vortex into a hidden alley full of garbage.

A stray cat foraging in the trash let out a vicious meow, its fur standing on end.

"We're not leaving the city center?" Amber asked.

"No. We might be able to catch someone if we wait," Kui Xin said slowly.

Amber said, "That enemy you've been looking for? You mean he might be drawn here by all the commotion?"

"Yes," Kui Xin said.

"I see..." An ice-blue light lit up deep in Amber's pupils, and the foraging cat and a few small rats nearby fell under his control.

At his command, the little animals scattered and began searching for Depriver 777.

Adam was also using the Investigation Department's surveillance network to look for Augus.

From what Kui Xin knew of the guy, he couldn't stand being ordinary and was desperate to show off. To put it crudely, he was a shit-stirrer: he loved being where the action was and loved wrecking things.

Kui Xin's laser projections from the rooftops hadn't been signed, but only a handful of people were paying attention to the Floating Port City Nuclear Power Plant at this point, and Kui Xin happened to be one of them. Augus might put two and two together and come to take a look.

Kui Xin hacked straight into the security officers' internal comms channel and listened to their reports.

"Aerial sweep of Shady Boulevard complete. No suspicious persons."

"Pedestrian sweep of Silver Avenue complete. No suspicious persons."

"Checkpoint set up on West Crossroads to stop passing vehicles and pedestrians. Screening in progress."

"..."

She sifted through the messages patiently.

Time passed.

After fifteen minutes, Kui Xin finally heard a different kind of report on the Investigation Department's channel.

"Hey, stop. You need to be scanned." A security officer's voice came over the channel, followed by the click of a gun being cocked as the officer raised his weapon. "I said stop, or I'm authorized to shoot you d—"

"Bzzt... bzzt..."

The channel abruptly turned into a crackle of static. The officer on the other end had gone silent.

Kui Xin's heart skipped. "Where?"

Adam said, "White Peacock Plaza, about eight hundred meters from here. The officer's body camera caught some footage. A white man passed the checkpoint and was stopped for a check, and then the feed cut out. His build matches the reconstruction you gave me, but his face was covered."

Kui Xin was seventy or eighty percent sure. A sudden urgency welled up in her, and she wanted to get there right away to confirm it.

"Let's go!" She turned and had taken only a few steps when she stopped and faced Amber. "No, you can't come with me. You probably don't have the edge in close combat, and your abilities don't give you one either."

Kui Xin slipped the heavy equipment bag off her shoulder and pushed it into Amber's arms. The bag, which had been covered by her optical camouflage, became visible for an instant before Amber's optical camouflage quickly covered it.

"Can you use a K80 long-range sniper rifle?" she asked.

"Of course I can. I got top marks in every firearms evaluation at Mechanical Dawn." Amber slung the bag over his shoulder. "You want me to take a long-range sniper position?"

"Yes. I'm counting on you. Keep your distance." Kui Xin pointed at his earpiece. "Follow Mirror's directions."

"Hello, Amber. This is Mirror." Adam's voice reached his ear, altered and processed. "From here on, please follow my directions. I'll plan your firing positions and escape routes."

Amber was taken aback.

He had always known that the Sea Without Light had a member called "Mirror," but he had never dealt with them. "Mirror" never showed themselves, and Amber only knew from the odd remark Kui Xin made that this member handled the technical side of things.

He set his doubts aside and said, "Hello. I'll do as you say."

If Kui Xin trusted this person, he should be able to work with them without worrying.

Kui Xin didn't take off her optical camouflage button. Her foot kicked a soda can at the mouth of the alley, and that was how Amber knew she was leaving.

"Fer-de-lance," Amber called after her. "Good luck..."

Kui Xin glanced back, surprised, gave a soft "mm," and left the alley.

Amber didn't waste any more time either. "Where are the good sniping spots, Mirror?"

"I'd advise against anything too tall, or escaping will be awkward." Adam spoke quickly but clearly. "About two hundred meters from here there's a cube-shaped sports center, sixty-five meters tall. The view is fairly open. From the top floor you can see White Peacock Plaza through the gaps between buildings, and at the right angle you'll have a shot."

"That'll do, then." Shouldering the heavy equipment bag, Amber nimbly made his way past toppled dumpsters and wire fencing and broke into a run.

As a soldier carefully trained by Mechanical Dawn, Amber was in no way inferior to the Investigation Department's security officers in any respect, and even surpassed them. Physical stamina wasn't his strong suit, but he was still several times fitter than an ordinary person.

By the time he reached the sports center there was a light sweat on his forehead. Following Adam's instructions, he immediately smashed open a side door and dashed up the staff stairwell to the top. It took him only five minutes to climb more than ten floors.

When he reached the roof, the night wind blew past and he felt a chill.

Without pausing for breath, he set down the equipment bag, unzipped it, pulled out a black case and began assembling the rifle.

The precision parts locked together, and a weapon capable of taking lives was in his hands. He loaded the rounds one by one, set up the bipod, searched for a firing angle and adjusted the readings on the electronic scope.

Amber's gaze crossed nearly a kilometer, and his muzzle locked onto White Peacock Plaza.

The plaza's electronic musical fountain was still running, its jets of water lit up in different colors. On ordinary days it played deep, soothing music, and shoppers liked to stop here and enjoy the grand musical fountain.

But now the fountain's peaceful scene had been ruined.

Patrol cars were heaped together in a jumble, as if something had twisted them apart out of thin air. Mangled bodies lay in the fountain, and blood had stained much of the pool red.

Kui Xin had reached the area, but she didn't get any closer.

Security officers patrolling nearby had already arrived as backup, but they hadn't seen a thing except the bodies of their comrades.

"Did I miss him? Is he already gone?" Kui Xin thought hard.

"No, it won't be that simple," she quickly decided. "He has to be prowling somewhere nearby..."

She quietly slipped back into cover, warily sweeping her surroundings with her eyes, and switched on her portable scanner to sweep the area.

Augus had "Shadow Realm," which let him hide his body inside a separate shadow space, so the scanner wouldn't be much use. Kui Xin was only using it just in case.

She shifted position slowly and kept searching the area.

About ten minutes later, as she was moving forward, she suddenly heard a soft laugh.

"So it really is you," said a familiar voice. "When I saw the words in the sky, I wondered who could be sticking their nose in. Could it be you?"

Augus stepped out of the shadows with a smile. "I guessed right. It's you after all."
