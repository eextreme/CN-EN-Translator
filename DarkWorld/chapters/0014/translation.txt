Night Cicada really was a complicated man... Kui Xin thought as she surfaced from the memories.

Then again, it wasn't surprising. Someone in his position, with powers like his, was bound to have an unusual background and an unusual past.

Reading Night Cicada's memories felt completely different from reading anyone else's. Everyone's memories were tangled up with their emotions, and the ones that burned deepest into memory tended to be the extreme ones. Those emotions hit Kui Xin's own mind hard, leaving her with splitting headaches and bouts of dizziness.

Four Leaves' memories had felt as bland as water. He had no strong emotions and no deep memories, so she'd gotten through them without any trouble. With Night Cicada's memories, though, Kui Xin had felt a contradiction.

The first half of his memories burned like fire, full of powerful emotion and searing hatred. The second half, however, was calm, close to how Four Leaves' had felt. The more Night Cicada went through, the less emotion his memories held... He was all grins and jokes on the surface, but inside he was as cold as a stone. His feelings never flared the way they used to.

Kui Xin had been surprised to learn that Night Cicada had gone through brutal experiments too, because he'd never shown the slightest sympathy for the artificial humans. Only when she reached the end of his memories did she understand.

Night Cicada had gone numb.

He had numbed himself and gone from victim to victimizer.

Kui Haidong had told him, "Other people used to hold the power of life and death over you. Now you can be that kind of person too, in control of your own life and the lives of others."

Just as Kui Haidong had said, Night Cicada became one of the people on top, and he felt nothing when he took a life... He was human, but his human feelings kept fading.

Kui Xin rubbed her forehead and tried to calm a mind crowded with jumbled memories.

She sat in her chair, lost in thought, and didn't move for a long time.

"Did you get any important intel?" Adam asked. "Why have you been quiet for so long?"

"I got important intel," Kui Xin said. "I also got a warning."

"Care to share?" Adam asked.

"There's not much to say... Night Cicada's life showed me how far someone can fall when they can't control their own fate," Kui Xin said. "He's a cautionary tale. Luckily, I'm not like him."

She stretched, and as she took off her heavy gear, it suddenly struck her that she and Night Cicada were actually a lot alike.

Night Cicada had chosen to numb himself and obey Eve. Kui Xin had made a choice too: she'd chosen to draw a clear line between herself and the Second World. Night Cicada had turned a blind eye to the suffering artificial humans, and Kui Xin had deliberately shut off her feelings, refusing to get attached to anyone in the Second World.

Her hands slowly stopped working at the gear. She asked abruptly, "Adam, do you think I've changed since the beginning? Be blunt."

Kui Xin waited for Adam's verdict. She guessed it would say something like, "You've grown colder and more calculating. You've eliminated one enemy after another and kept walking the path you planned without wavering."

But after waiting a few seconds, she heard the exact opposite from Adam.

"You don't feel as distant to me anymore," it replied. "When we first met, you were too cold and too rational. Now that I know you, I know you aren't completely cold. You spared Four Leaves, you recruited Amber, you put up with them, and you agreed to my request. You used to feel very far away to me. Now you're not so far."

Kui Xin hadn't expected that answer. For a moment it actually left her stunned.

"So that's how you see me?" she blurted.

She'd had a reason for asking Adam. Adam had watched her grow. She'd met Adam the moment she entered "Crimson Earth," and it had been there for her entire timeline, from the day she entered the game until now. Nothing in the Second World knew her better.

"I didn't understand you at first, but I do now," Adam said. "I know you're a flawed person, Kui Xin. You aren't especially kind, and most of what you do comes down to self-interest. But you have plenty of good qualities too. Resilience, for one. And... honesty."

"I never thought I'd be associated with a word as wholesome as 'honesty' in your mind," Kui Xin said.

"No, what I mean is that you aren't a hypocrite, and you don't make excuses for yourself. Self-interest is self-interest, desire is desire, the urge to kill is the urge to kill, and the urge for revenge is the urge for revenge. You don't dress these things up as something else or cloak them in false virtue," Adam said. "That's what I admire most about you. You hold my attention more than anyone else."

"I'd have thought you'd prefer someone like Shu Xuyao," Kui Xin said.

"The two aren't in conflict. Tastes and standards come in many forms," Adam said. "I like a ray of light in a dark world, and I like people full of flaws. Nothing in this world is perfect, and flaws make a person feel real. What I'm trying to say is... you're a real person, Kui Xin."

"All right... your answer wasn't what I expected," Kui Xin said.

"What answer were you hoping for?" Adam asked.

"Nothing," Kui Xin said. "I just got a little sentimental all of a sudden."

She looked back on the road she had traveled. It wasn't a long one, and she hadn't been on it for long, yet so much had happened and she'd made so many choices.

Just as Night Cicada had become someone completely different from his old self, she too had turned into someone she barely recognized.

"As for the important intel, there's a lot of it. A huge amount," Kui Xin said, getting down to business. "I know the coordinates of most of Mechanical Dawn's bases, and I know where the players and Obsidian are being held... I also learned that Night Cicada and Ghost seem to have known each other for a long time."

But Night Cicada hadn't known Ghost was still alive. Kui Xin had only worked out his identity from "You Wang's" ability. He most likely hadn't died and was now working for the Resistance.

The Resistance and Mechanical Dawn were enemies, so without ever knowing it, Night Cicada had become the sworn enemy of his childhood friend. As for Ghost... the Resistance had eyes inside Mechanical Dawn, and with an ability like Night Cicada's, he was bound to become one of their targets for elimination... What a tangled story.

Kui Xin gave Adam a brief rundown of Night Cicada's memories.

"The situation is far more complicated than I imagined," Adam said. "The Federation's testing grounds are scattered everywhere, and the projects they run differ quite a bit. If Eve can reach into a place like that, it must have gained access to the testing base. It may even have devoured the AI there and taken over as the base's administrator."

Kui Xin frowned.

Yu Hanxue was still in there. Kui Xin didn't know which testing base she was in, but if it overlapped with the territory Eve controlled, she'd be in even greater danger.

There was no use dwelling on it now. As long as Yu Hanxue stayed undercover, she would be reasonably safe, and Kui Xin couldn't do much to help her anytime soon anyway.

"We've got the most important thing. It's time to plan our next move," Kui Xin said. "We'll split up. Lancet's squad goes to Black Sea City to start the business, and I'll go to Floating Port City to finish my mission."

"You're going alone?" Adam asked.

"Going alone obviously won't work. I need to bring a helper," Kui Xin said. "Amber's good. He's the one. Four Leaves isn't ready yet, so Perigon Falcon can look after him, and Ash will team up with Perigon Falcon to cover his weakness in combat."

With that, the Sea Without Light's squads began to take shape.

Ash, Perigon Falcon and Four Leaves made up Team One.

Lancet, Wei Li and Golden Ring made up Team Two.

Kui Xin herself and Amber made up Team Three... Adam, as "Mirror," the Sea Without Light's elusive hacker whom no one ever saw, also belonged to Team Three.

"Is Team One going to Black Sea City with Team Two?" Adam asked.

Kui Xin hesitated for a moment, then said, "Yes. We're too short on people right now, and splitting our fighting strength isn't a good idea. It's time to give up on a backwater like White Whale City. The gangs and businesses the Sea Without Light took over can all be sold off. Or not. We have money now anyway, so we could just use Soul-Bewitching Voice to control a few obedient people and have them run the gangs."

Then there was Samanto, the Special Intelligence Division mole. That wasn't a big problem either. With the spatial vortex, she could open a few vortexes and send Amber back to keep brainwashing him.

The distance between White Whale City and Black Sea City wasn't a big problem anymore either. Any problem the spatial vortex could solve wasn't a problem at all.

Kui Xin could finally break free of her shackles and show what she was capable of.

"We'll carry out the relocation tomorrow." Kui Xin flopped down heavily on the bed and muttered her plans under her breath. "The raid on Eve's stronghold has to happen as soon as possible too. It has to be fast. Eve already knows Night Cicada is dead, and it can guess I'll get his memories. Luckily I was a step ahead of it. It was too busy working Night Cicada to the bone evacuating the branch to bother giving him a blood transfusion... As long as Eve isn't stupid, it'll move its people and leave me grabbing at air."

She forced her eyes open. "Actually, the best time to raid the base is tonight! There's no way it can relocate in time tonight."

Adam's voice was full of obvious exasperation. "Don't push yourself so hard, Kui Xin. You're clearly not back at your peak. If you go now, you're more likely to run into disaster. How is an exhausted fighter supposed to take on Mechanical Dawn's combat personnel?"

"...You're right. If I go now, I could easily get myself killed there." Kui Xin lay back down on the bed. "I need to recover fast... There's still so much waiting for me..."

She finally fell into a deep sleep.

...

A new day arrived. It was now half past ten in the morning.

Lancet, Wei Li and Golden Ring had arrived early at the designated meeting point.

"Did Fer-de-lance say what we're supposed to do?" Golden Ring asked.

"No." Wei Li shook her head.

Golden Ring looked Lancet over. "Didn't you sleep last night? Why do you keep yawning?"

Lancet wiped the reflexive tears from the corners of his eyes and said mournfully, "I spent all night writing a business plan..."

Before long, a deep-blue spatial vortex opened up beside the three of them.

Lancet looked up and saw a stranger step out of the vortex, a man in a black combat suit and a black mask... A man? He frowned. Something was off. The newcomer didn't seem to be a grown man at all. From his build, he looked more like a minor.

Wei Li's face changed the moment she saw him. No matter what she tried, she couldn't activate her mind-reading. It was as if her ability had been erased out of thin air.

All three of them sensed something was wrong.

Fer-de-lance came out of the vortex next, followed by three more people, both men and women, all in combat suits and masks.

"Let me introduce everyone. This is Four Leaves." Kui Xin pointed to each of them as she went. "This is Amber. You've met him before. The one on the left is Perigon Falcon, and the one on the right is Ash. You'll most likely be working together on missions from now on."

Wei Li looked at Four Leaves and asked carefully, "And what is Four Leaves' ability?"

Four Leaves didn't even spare her a glance. To him, she might as well have been thin air.

"His ability has to do with nullification," Kui Xin replied. "It's a necessary precaution."

Wei Li had her answer, and she didn't look the least bit resentful at not being trusted. She knew she was a new recruit with an unusual ability. Until she had contributed something to the organization, it was only natural that they'd keep their guard up around her. Four Leaves' ability was aimed at her.

Four Leaves didn't say a word or let his eyes wander. He just stared straight down at the ground.

He Kangshi had briefed him beforehand: don't speak unless you have to, and don't make any unnecessary moves. Keep up that big-shot aura, and people will naturally be in awe of you and behave themselves.

Four Leaves had said, "Won't that seem really cold? You taught me to be polite..."

"Be polite to the people close to you and be wary of strangers," He Kangshi had said. "They're not close to us yet!"

He Kangshi had worried himself sick. Four Leaves was too much like a little kid. He was smart enough, but his emotional intelligence was a bit lacking, and He Kangshi was afraid he'd give the game away and reveal what their side really had. So he had taken every precaution he could think of and drilled the instructions into him over and over.

"Your mission is to get a foothold in Black Sea City, set up a company, and build a base there. You'll split into two squads with different jobs. Lancet's squad will handle building the company, and Ash's squad will handle setting up the base," Kui Xin said. "Once you're in Black Sea City, there'll be no fake identities and nowhere to stay. You'll have to do everything yourselves. But there are two things I can give you."

She raised her hand, and a spatial vortex opened parallel to the ground. Several crates of ammunition and firearms dropped out of it.

Then she tossed Lancet a black communicator, and he caught it neatly.

"That should be enough weapons. The account linked to this communicator's number has three million in startup funds," Kui Xin said. "The rest is up to you... You can think of it as another test."

A bigger spatial vortex yawned open behind them. Kui Xin gestured for them to gather up the weapons and gear on the ground and step through.

After several jumps in a row, they arrived near their destination.

Black Sea City lay right in front of them. Kui Xin was seeing this strange yet familiar city again, but she gave it only a single glance before quickly turning to look at the people beside her.

"Go," she said, looking at them. "I want to see how far you can take this."

He Kangshi spoke up first. "We won't let you down, Big Sis Fer-de-lance!"

Su Rong said, "Leave it to us."

Golden Ring grinned, flashing his teeth. "I'll do my very best to help!"

Wei Li said, "I'll do as I'm told."

"The business plan is already written. I'll carry it out as planned," Lancet said, all seriousness.

A spatial vortex opened behind Kui Xin. Four Leaves knew she was leaving. He held himself back and just barely kept up his cold big-shot act, managing only a short "Goodbye!"

Amber glanced at Four Leaves, and Four Leaves hurriedly added, "You too. Goodbye."

So he was just an afterthought? Under his mask, Amber's expression turned helpless, and he found it a little funny. Then he turned and followed Kui Xin into the vortex.
